## Introduction

Not really feeling your character's build? Accidentally broke your oath? Told your god to go fuck themselves? We've got rules for that.

Respeccing is handled on a case-by-case basis. The gold and downtime days required represent the time spent learning these new skills along with the gold spent to buy new materials, hire tutors, or otherwise research this change. To respecc a character, please open a Character Ticket in ⁠[🎫-submit-a-ticket](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239341873873551370).

## Respeccing After Level 4

Respeccing after level 4 will have an associated gold cost. The gold and downtime cost required to make the change is detailed as such:

### Changing Subclass:

**500 gp** and **1 downtime day** per level.   
*The only exception is for classes such as Paladin, Cleric, or Warlock where significant story events may cause a change in alignment, oath, domain, or patron.*

### Changing Class:

**1000 gp** and **2 downtime days** per level.   
*This payment can be handwaved with significant story changes to a character, such as a Ranger creating a deeper bond with nature and becoming a Druid, a Fighter taking an Oath to become a Paladin, a Cleric turning to a new source of power to become a Warlock, etc.)*

### Changing a Feat/ASI:

**2000 gp** and **4 downtime days** per feat   
*This includes changing the stat increase for half-feats such as skill expert.*

### Changing Racial ASIs:

To change the allocation of your racial ASIs the cost is **1000gp** and **1 downtime day** for all of them.

### Swapping Ability Scores:

To swap your base ability scores it is **2000 gp** and **2 down time days** per pair, **3000 gp** and **3 down time days** if swapping three scores.

### Changing Spells:

To change your selected spells for classes such as Sorcerer, Bard, or Warlock, it is **1500 gp** and **1 down time day** per spell.

### Changing a Race:

You cannot change your race except by Reincarnation or very special circumstances. Such circumstances could include becoming a Dhampir, Reborn, or Hexblood. If you wish to change your race, please open a Story ticket in ⁠🎫-submit-a-ticket.

### Note:

- Your character is barred from adventuring while a respeccing ticket is open.
- Sheet Managers withhold the right to deny a respecc for any reason.
- Significant changes in class will require a suitable story reason.
- You may not request a respec for a character more than once in a two-week period.

## Free Respecc Before T3

All players are given **1 free respecc** before reaching level 11 (Tier 3). This Respecc still follows all the rules and important notes listed above! Please use this respecc within reason; if you are not having fun with your build or something significant changes with your character, then that is a great reason to use your respecc! On the other hand, wanting to respecc because you happened to acquire a cool new item is something we will likely make you pay for (getting a Belt of Giants Strength and wanting to dump your strength score is not a valid use of your free respecc). We are adding this option because we know many players are new and don't always know what they are building when they start. Please don't abuse this free respecc, because its something to help players find enjoyment in their builds, not something to minmax!
