## Introduction

Should your character perish outside the city, your friends might be able to retrieve the remains. In order to do this, a **Death/Retirement Ticket** must be created to ensure no unfair advantage or violation of the rules.

Your character, post-mortem, may not inform other characters of the body's location.

You may not use OOC meta-knowledge to find a character's remains - You must have gotten this information in character.

The retrieval party must have OOC consent to search for the remains.

How to Retrieve a Casualty's Remains:

- So long as you follow the above caveats your party may search for remains using a Survival check. The DCs are as follows:
- Location Immediately next to the city and it's sub-threads (I.E. ⁠Verdant Expanse) - DC 20
- Location one channels or more away from the city (I.E. ⁠Cerro Foothills, Echoing Canopy, etc) - DC 25

***This check may only be done once. Ping Moderator before performing the check.***

Remains cannot be retrieved if the character died at a Hoard

## Condition of Remains

Remains may be in varying states of decay or dismemberment. Some of this may change based on monster behaviour. Characters who die to monsters which their entry states anything about devouring bodies completely, using bodies for other purposes, animating dead, or warping a creature, etc, or have spells/abilities on their stat block that do that, will have their remains found in that condition. (E.g. Only bones and scraps of flesh would be left of a character that died to gnolls or carnivores, deceased characters will be turned into zombies by a necromancer, bandits would take everything on their person, etc)

**Otherwise, roll 1d50 to determine the condition of the body...**

1 - 5 | Perfect condition aside from wounds received in battle

6 - 10 | Missing Arm or Hand Unless revived by a spell that restores missing body parts... You can no longer hold anything with two hands, and you can hold only a single object at a time.

11 - 15 | Missing Leg or Foot Unless revived by a spell that restores missing body parts... Your speed on foot is halved, and you must use a cane or crutch to move unless you have a peg leg or other prosthesis. You fall prone after using the Dash action. You have disadvantage on Dexterity checks made to balance.

16 - 20 | Missing an Eye(s) - Roll 1d2 to determine one or both. Unless revived by a spell that restores missing body parts... You have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight and on ranged attack rolls. Magic such as the regenerate spell can restore the lost eye. If you have no eyes left after sustaining this injury, you’re blinded.

21 - 25 | Missing Head

26 - 35 | Missing minor bits of flesh from scavengers and other wildlife

36 - 40 | Nothing left but bones/scraps of flesh

41 - 45 | Nothing left but vestiges of equipment

46 - 50 | Eviscerated/Destroyed/Missing vital internal organs

Please keep the body's condition in mind when choosing your revival means. Regenerate can be used to restore missing limbs and eyes, but not vital organs or heads.

## Money Left on Remains

Sometimes scavengers and looters will take money and equipment from corpses. When a body is found, determine the items and gold remaining on their person using the following guide:

Roll 1d100. On a 10 or below, all equipment and money is missing. Otherwise,

Tally up the number of magic items you have. Roll a dice equal to that number to see how many are missing and remove them from your inventory in the order they are listed in !bag.

Then roll 2d50. The result of that is the % of money lost.

Players with homes may make a !bag for home storage and should consider using a custom counter or separate bag for their “bank account” or gold kept at home. Items and gold in storage are not lost when a body is recovered.

## Non-Death Character "Deaths"

Some mechanics in D&D may not cause death, but may effectively permanently remove your character from play. For the most part, the following mechanics will cause you to lose your character and roll a new one. The following count as permanent character death:

- Lycanthropy
- Vampirism
- Ceremorphosis
- Life time imprisonment
- Soul snatched by a fiend (i.e. Pact, Hellfire Weapon, etc)
- Any permanent warping into another non-playable creature

As with character death, you/your character’s friends have two weeks to either seek out Wish or other means of reverting/saving your character. After that time period, the character is lost forever.

### Lycanthropy

For one afflicted with Lycanthropy, the curse takes 1 real time week to fully incubate. Within that time, a PC should seek out a cure.

After the week is up and the character succumbs to Lycanthropy, control of that character is lost. A new character must be rolled.
