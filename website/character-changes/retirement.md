## Maximum Characters / Alt Accounts

Our XP Bot Scandroid can store up to 10 character slots. Players can have a total of 5 Active Characters and 5 Inactive Characters.

**Active Characters** - Can be switched between at will and played as usual. They earn xp and downtime.

**Inactive Characters** - Cannot be switched to at will and cannot roleplay, gain xp, or gain downtime while Inactive.

**Retired Characters** - These are previous characters that have been completely removed from Scandroid character slots. They can be brought back by following the rules in the Retirement section.

Your characters cannot adventure with each other or swap items, money, spells, etc. Your characters can RP with each other if there is another player present and included in the RP, but they cannot have extended 1 on 1 conversations together (AKA RPing with yourself). If your characters do RP together, avoid using meta knowledge to benefit your other character. I.E.: Character A knows a secret that would be beneficial for Character B to know. Unless Character A has a reason to share this secret, do not share it with Character B just for the OOC benefit.

Your characters can **NEVER** share a combat or initiative together.

## Level Rewards

When your character reaches certain levels they gain a Level Reward. This means that you can bring in a new character with more benefits. Use the information below to determine your level benefits. **You can only claim Level Rewards ONCE PER CHARACTER.**

I.E.: I claim my Level 10 reward when Mika is Level 14. Therefore I can no longer claim the Level 16 or Level 20 reward.

**Level 10 Reward**

New level 5 character starts with basic gear, 2,000 Gold, and **one** of the following:

- Rare item
- Two Uncommon items
- Additional 2,000 gold

**Level 1****6** **Reward**

New level 8 character starts with basic gear, 5,000 Gold, 30 Renown to their Faction of choice (not Universal), and **one** of the following:

- Two Rare items
- Three Uncommon items
- Additional 5,000 gold

**Level** **20** **Reward**

New level 10 character starts with basic gear, 10,000 Gold, 30 downtime days, 50 Renown to their Faction of choice (not Universal), and **one** of the following:

- One Very Rare item
- Two Rare Items and One Uncommon
- Additional 10,000 gold

This magic item must be from one of the approved books found in #📗-character-creation and cannot have any of the following properties:

- Sentient (Can be flexible on this within reason)
- Increase an ability score
- Quest only items (Dragon Wrath Weapon, Horizon Puzzle Cube, etc)

A Sheet Manager has the right to refuse any item.

## Changing Your Active Characters

If you would like to make one of your Inactive characters Active, you can use ⁠🛠-character-set-up to make this change. Do the following:

- If you already have 5 Active Slots filled you will need to declare which character will go Inactive.
- Reactivate your characters downtime clock and likewise turn off the downtime clock of any character going Inactive.
- Ping a @Sheet Manager then resume play. You do not need to wait for approval as long as you've clearly stated which characters are changing status and have shown the change in downtime clocks.
- Update your name to reflect who your active characters are.

**Please do not abuse this system by constantly switching in and out characters within short time periods or the mod team will have to enforce a time limit on swaps.**

## Bringing a Character out of Retirement

Retiring a character is meant to be permanent. Each player gets **one chance** per character to bring them out of retirement if they realize they want to play them again. **Once that one-time option is used, the character cannot be unretired again.**

When a character is brought out of retirement, they are the same level as when they left, and retain any XP progress to the next level so long as the player can provide the logs (screenshot, keeping the character's xp stored in Scandroid, a message link, etc). If this is not provided they will lose any XP progress to the next level.

Eg: If your character retires at level 5 with 80% of the XP required to get to level 6 and you can't find what their xp % was before, they will return from retirement with 0% xp at level 5.

If bringing a character out of retirement, all equipment, stats, abilities, feats, and spell, must be the same as when they went into retirement unless a respec was discussed via a ticket. It is highly recommended you keep your original character sheet on D&D Beyond or Avrae to avoid any accidental changes.
