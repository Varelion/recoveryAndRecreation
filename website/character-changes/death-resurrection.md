## Introduction

Character death happens all the time and can be quite upsetting when a lot of time and love has been invested in their story. It's okay to feel sad or angry, but its not okay to take that out on other players. Take a moment to collect yourself, and then **open a Death/Retirement Ticket** so that staff can record the death, help list out your options, and possibly run a story so that you might come back.

If you believe a misunderstanding, unfair application of the rules, bad faith DMing or malicious actions of another character resulted in your character's death, please contact the support team using the ⁠⁠[🎫-submit-a-ticket](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239341873873551370) function and a Moderator will investigate it for you.

## Revival & Resurrection

We will be following the DMG's statement on reviving dead player characters, which states:

- A soul can’t be returned to life if it doesn’t wish to be. A soul knows the name, alignment, and patron deity (if any) of the character attempting to revive it and might refuse to return on that basis.

You cannot revive NPCs or monsters. Once they are dead they are dead, unless a quest/plot point is discussed with a Moderator ahead of time.

The soul will know the alignment of the one who revived them. If the soul knows the revived character knows. The alignment and deity the caster can be shielded through a Ring of Mind Shielding or similar magic item.

Speak with Dead specifically states that the soul is NOT returned to the body. As stated in the spell:

- This spell doesn’t return the creature’s soul to its body, only its animating spirit. Thus, the corpse can’t learn new information, doesn’t comprehend anything that has happened since it died, and can’t speculate about future events.
- This also explains why it can be done to those unwilling to be resurrected, whereas revival cannot.

A willing soul is required for Revivify. This is because:

- The DMG has specified this as aforementioned.
- Characters will not be permitted to attempt to revive unwilling dead characters. It creates too much confusion. From an OOC standpoint, the soul is likely unwilling because the player does not want to play their deceased character again.
- Revived dead characters will not become NPCs. Only the DMs can decide who is an NPC and control those NPCs beyond simple interactions i.e. Ordering a drink at the tavern. A player character requires the original player to control them.

### Soul Rot

Coming back from death is quite the ordeal, both in mind, body, and soul. Repeatedly crossing this boundary has lasting consequences.

**!soulrot [Death Toll] [Wisdom Mod] [Situational +/-]**

Upon resurrection, players roll a percentile die using the !soulrot command. This roll cannot be modified by any abilities or items. The results of the Soul Rot are determined by the character's Death Toll as detailed below:

- Death Toll = # of Deaths
- Mi - Minor Soul Rot
- Mo - Moderate Soul Rot
- Se - Severe Soul Rot

## Restrictions on Revivals

### Wish for Revivals

Wish cannot Revive people. This applies to Revivify, Raise Dead, Resurrection, Reincarnate, and Clone.

### Spell Components for Revival Spells

Material components for reviving the dead have become increasingly difficult to come by. Players cannot purchase the material components required for Raise Dead and above (Including Clone and not including Reincarnate) at the markets. They must acquire them by helping the various factions present or by finding them in a hoard.

### Items Cost Diamonds

Resurrection items cost the spell components (☆) that are listed in the spell being used, even if the items usually do not (i.e. Cauldron of Rebirth). This is bypassed only if the item being used says it covers the cost of the spell components (i.e. Ruby Weave Gem) or you have a class feature what covers the cost (i.e. Wizard Blood Magic: Blood Channeling).

- Rod of Resurrection: If using a 5k Diamond with a Rod of Resurrection, there is no 1d20 destruction roll. If you choose to use the rod without a diamond, roll for destruction as usual.

### Ruby Weave Gem Limitation

Ruby Weave Gem can no longer be used to cast True Resurrection.

## Limits Of Revival

### Time Limit on Revivals

Once your character has passed away, you have two weeks to either revive your character or for there to be a Story Ticket opened for a “resurrection quest”. After the two-week mark, you cannot resurrect a dead character.

## Reincarnation

Reincarnation will likely change some aspects of your character including allocation of racial ASI and other features. Please open a Character Ticket in the event of Reincarnation.

Keep in mind Reincarnation is a very random spell. Blaming the caster or player of the caster for an unfavorable race will result in a warning.
