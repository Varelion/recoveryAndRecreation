# Tales of Nymrathis

*West Marches - Play by Post - D&D 5e*

## Welcome to Tales of Nymrathis

We are an 21+ D&D 5e West Marches server. We value a safe, fun, and fair game environment, and as such have a rules to facilitate this vision of our community. Please read these rules before you create a character and begin playing. Additionally, consult the rules as you play before consulting the staff when you have questions.

While we have more specific rules detailed on other pages, please keep these general rules in mind as you play. Failure to abide by the rules may result in a warning, time out, kick or ban depending on the severity and the harm of the violation.

## General Rules

1️⃣ **This is an 21+ server. Adults only.**

RP in this server may deal with sexual, violent, or sensitive topics. All players must be over 21 to join. Any indication you are less than 21 years old and you will be banned from the server.

2️⃣ **Be respectful.**

This is a game, people come here to have fun. Moreover, our staff volunteer their personal time to make this place fun and engaging. OOC, do not act hostile or passive aggressive to others. Do not cause disruptive arguments or resort to name calling, slander, or disrespect. A disagreement is perfectly normal and healthy, hostility is not. Excessive disrespect or hostility will result in a warning or ban. Please keep all intense forms of debate off the server (such as about religion, politics, or current world events) as this is meant to be a place of relaxation.

**3️⃣ Homophobia, transphobia, racism, or sexism will not be tolerated.**

Fantasy versions of these topics may be explored in a respectful manner IC, with the consent of all players involved in the RP. OOC homophobia, transphobia, racism, or sexism will not be tolerated.

**4️⃣ Respect the staff.**

Both Moderators and Storytellers are here to make a fun and fair experience, while still working within the constraints of a text based westmarches. Their decision on rulings is final, and they will be treated with respect. They are your Dungeon Masters. If you need help, please use the right channels such as ⁠[❓-questions-and-help](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456129673179172954) and ⁠[🎫-submit-a-ticket](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456131236228759694). Under no circumstances are staff to be direct messaged about server issues. Do not demand immediate rulings and use the tools we have in place. Failure to respect the staff’s time or privacy will result in a warning.

**5️⃣ Consent is necessary and agency is key.**

All PVP (Player versus Player) must be done with consent. Any issues with another player violating this rule can be brought up to the moderator. Consent also applies to sexual or romantic roleplay between characters. Consent must be obtained from IC and OOC interactions. Keep in mind: While this server is 18+, it is not the server for egregious depictions of violence, gore, torture, or abuse. Even solo RP is not the place to explore these topics.

**6️⃣ Do not metagame.**

Metagaming is using a strategy, information, or acting in a way that your character shouldn't be capable or aware of. You cannot metagame either RP or encounters. Your character must have the precise means to know information in order for you as a player to utilize what you know out of character. Metagaming additionally includes: Running hoard commands to peek at what is in them (even if you have been there before), looking up global variables to see all possible dynamic encounters, using metaknowledge about a monster’s statblock that your character could not possibly know. If you’re looking to know what you might be up against, you can always ask around IC!

**7️⃣ In Character =/= Out of Character.**

Please keep in mind that interactions IC (in-character) do not reflect the feelings or opinions of the player OOC (out of character). If there is any concern of bleeding over, please check with one another, or open a support ticket to help settle any disputes or hurt feelings. ⁠[⛑-safety-discussions](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456128112210214924) can likewise be used to discuss any concerns with one-another.

**8️⃣ Actions have consequences.**

This is a living world where the choices your character makes and the actions they take will have an effect on the world. They will become known for the things they do and say. Keep this in mind.

**9️⃣ Follow server specific rules.**

Please follow the posted character creation, travel, and shopping rules to ensure ease and fairness of play. We understand this format is a little different than a home game, but take the time to learn and follow the posted rules.

**1️⃣0️⃣ Follow the basic rules of D&D 5e.**

While some adjustments to the rules have been made for this format, the basic rules and lord of D&D 5e still apply. Dynamic encounters and exploration are largely player run. Please adhere to D&D 5E rules, lore, and monster behaviour when running encounters and play them fairly to the best of your ability. Any questions or ruling questions can be brought up with a @Monster Manual

**1️⃣1️⃣ Keep OOC chatter in RP channels to a minimum.**

XP is earned exclusively through posts to encourage quality RP, so please try and keep OOC chatter within channels to a minimum. No one-liners. No media content in RP channels. Please use ⁠⁠[💬-ooc-chat](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456141074727829524) or ⁠⁠[💠-bot-spam](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456164930435944596) for gifs, pictures, etc. Please react to posts with emojis rather than posting an emoji in RP channels.

**1️⃣2️⃣ Focus on the story, and keep your posts legible.**

This server focuses on writing and creative storytelling. Do not post one-liners or walls of text with no punctuation. Do use proper grammar and spelling, and follow RP formatting rules.

If moving to another area of town please link the thread within your post.

**1️⃣3️⃣ PVP rules do not protect your character from retaliation.**

Your character's actions do not go without consequences. PvP (Player versus Player) rules do not allow your character to act as they please without repercussions within the story from other characters, or from a Moderator. Acting mean and then hiding behind PvP rules isn't cool, nor is it allowed!

**1️⃣4️⃣ Have fun!**

D&D is after all, just a game. Take it easy, enjoy storytelling and clickity clacking dice, and don't ruin others' fun!
