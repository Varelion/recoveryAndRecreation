## Approved and Banned Content

All WoTC Official classes, races, feats and backgrounds are allowed, including official MTG (Ravinca, Theros), and Eberron.

**Important Note About Spells:**

- Spell that stipulate "as chosen by the GM/DM" or the DM chooses/rolls/etc should go through the Moderators first to avoid world-breaking consequences or unfair gameplay. This can be done in ⁠[❓-questions-and-help](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456129673179172954) or privately for larger, more complicated spells/features such as Divine Intervention and Wish via the ⁠[🎫-submit-a-ticket](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456131236228759694) channel.

## Getting Started

- Stats are rolled in the ⁠[🎲-character-rolls](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456187241029435617) channel, then indicated which stats you will choose.
- We use fixed (average) hp as on this server.
- All characters get 1 free feat to start. This includes feats given as a part of a background, such as Strixhaven and Spelljammer backgrounds.
- Players start at Level 5 with starting equipment and 200 GP.
- Tasha's Customized Origins and Optional Class Features are permitted.
- All characters must be over 18 or an adult for their species. This is an adult server. All characters will be adults.

While characters can be of any alignment, it is important that this is a team game and your character should be able to play well with others. Characters that negatively impact the play experience of other characters are not allowed, and a moderator may speak to you privately if your character is seen as negatively impacting the play experience. Example of this are: Characters that provoke fights while adventuring when other party members are not hostile, intentionally abandoning party members, or causing excessive IC drama or hostility that is detrimental to the RP experience.

Villain PCs are not allowed. The GMs maintain control of every villain. More info in [⁠PvP & Villainous PCs](the-basics/pvp-villainous-pcs.md). If your character cannot follow these rules it is inappropriate for the server.

Characters are to be original. No meme characters or characters that are explicitly a character from media. (I.e. Naruto, Harry Potter). Characters can be inspired by media, but should not be rip-offs. Additionally, if a character dies, it is not permitted to make a new one that is the exact same as the old one (I.e. John 2).

Completed characters' description and portrait can be submitted to [character profiles](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456414212686610556).

Character set up spam including updating your description can be put into ⁠[🛠-character-set-up](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456189092688564257). Then, ping **@Sheet Manager** for approval. Make sure to turn on your Downtime Tracker to accumulate downtime days by entering **!****timer** on in one of the spam channels.

## Maximum Characters / Alt Accounts

Players begin with one (1) active character slot. These slots increase at certain capstones:

- Reach Level 8 on One Character: Two (2) Active character slots
- Reach Level 11 on One Character: Three (3) Active character slots.
- Reach level 16 on One Character: Four (4) Active character slots
- +1 Bonus Slot - Server Boost (This slot will be active only while the boost is **active**)

The maximum amount of active character slots any player can have, including the server boost slot, is **five (5)**.

Your characters cannot adventure with each other or swap items, money, spells, etc.

Your characters can RP with each other if there is another player present and included in the RP, but they cannot have extended 1 on 1 conversations together (AKA RPing with yourself). If your characters do RP together, avoid using meta knowledge to benefit your other character.

- I.E.: Character A knows a secret that would be beneficial for Character B to know. Unless Character A has a reason to share this secret, do not share it with Character B just for the OOC benefit.

Your characters can NEVER share a combat or initiative together.

## Ironman Characters

A new style of game play is being made available for those who want to do so. Everyone is being given an extra character slot reserved for an Ironman character to use if they desire a bit of an extra challenge when it comes to adventuring.

1. No trading or gifting. You can’t trade, give items/gold (with the exception of donating to public business portals), receive items, or shop at the player-run bazaar. You can only buy items from faction shops, NPC vendors, or hoard/dungeon loot. This includes crafting as well, you may craft items but other player characters aren’t able to assist with the crafting or provide you with monster parts.
2. Limited consumables. You can only use consumables you found or bought yourself. Allies can’t feed you potions, scrolls, tomes, or use consumables on you. Spells and class features (like Healing Word) are allowed.
3. Permadeath is default. If you die and don’t have your own plan (like Clone, Soul Cage, or a faction-based resurrection), you stay dead. No one else can assist with your resurrection.

3. Item restrictions. No using manuals, tomes, or magic items unless you found them yourself. No passing items to future characters. Dead characters’ gear is lost.

1. Declare Ironman status at character creation. Display the tag (IRON) in your tupper clearly. Breaking rules voids your Ironman status retroactively. Everyone will be allowed one Ironman character slot, it does not take up one of your traditional slots.
2. When traveling with other players characters they cannot be at a higher tier of play than your Ironman character. You can however travel with characters of the same tier or lower.
3. Your Ironman character starts at level 3, and can only earn xp when using said character. If you want to RP or adventure on a different character you must make sure to swap off of them so they are not gaining xp.
4. Faction Agent- since you’re primarily interacting with NPCs in terms of trade and deals, you are allowed to join a secondary faction. Faction renown earned will be worth double renown, same as your primary faction. You can go up to Rank 5 within your primary faction as normal but only up to Rank 3 in your secondary faction.
5. Rewards for storytellers/mods/questers for running quests or doing server work or bumping Disboard cannot be applied to Ironman characters.

## Using Scandroid

If you wish to have multiple characters on one discord account, you will want to familiarize yourself with these bot commands! Feel free to **@XP Manager** for help.  
  
Use **/xp** to check your own XP

Use **/edit_character** to change your character's name or portrait

Use **/set** to change which character is gaining XP

- I.E.: Kei's first character is Lumina, with an index number of 1, and Amica, with an index number of 2. To gain XP on Amica, she would use **/set 2**. To switch to Lumina, she would use **/set 1**

Use **/set** after advancing to a new tier to apply the proper tier role to your account.

Go to **/xp**, then click the 'Retire' button twice to retire a character. This will ping staff and inform them of the retirement.
