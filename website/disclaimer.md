# Disclaimer

## Changes to Rules

Rulings and changes need to happen all the time, and we try and make this as painless as possible. All new rulings or homebrew adjustments will be posted on[⁠📣announcements](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456313901103841331) before being updated here.

We understand that it is frustrating when rules change, especially ones that affect your character, spell selection, etc. Should any rule affect your play experience negatively, please submit a ticket and discuss an issue with a Moderator. The Moderator will ensure a fair retcon or adjustment is offered to your character allowing you to respec, change spells, or swap out magic items free of charge and as painlessly as possible. We ask that you understand and respect the unique issues that arise on a text-based, player-run, 24/7 West Marches servers and that all servers and D&D tables adjust rules from time to time.

Some rulings by Storytellers or DMs will be situation specific for a mid-adventure hiccup, then undergo a process where the staff will decided if the ruling will stay on the server. Please respec that some rulings are temporary and need to be made to facilitate the continuation of an adventure or quest, but may not be deemed suitable to stay on the server moving forward.

At the end of the day, the Moderators have final say over any and all rulings. Players who are excessively hostile or uncooperative will be spoken with and potentially removed from the server.
