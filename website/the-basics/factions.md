# Introduction to Factions

Factions are here to create more dimension to our server with new goals and rewards for players! Factions work off of a **Renown** system, which players can earn through quests, roleplay, and weekly missions to then spend that Renown through **Faction Shops**. Renown will be something given out as rewards when applicable and also something that can be requested via story tickets.

Factions will also occasionally put out larger quests outside of the static **Weekly Missions**. We want to encourage making groups and roleplaying these quests together when they come along!

Players are not locked into earning Renown from a single faction, however, whatever renown they earn from missions in the faction they have joined is doubled. The Renown you earn is a direct representation of your efforts to help that particular group. Additionally, **Universal Renown** can be used with any Faction, as a sign that your work was respected by all the Factions present.
