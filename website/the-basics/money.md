## Managing Your Inventory

This server uses the **!coin** and **!bag** command to manage inventories. Please note you will have to manually monitor your own purse and bag for items found and coin gained.

This server uses all denominations of coins from silver to gold. To add or subtract coins use !coin +/- # [denomination ie. sp or gp] To set up your bag use the !bag function.

For help, use the !bag help command, which will send a detailed list of bag commands to assist you.

### ❗Important❗

What is in your bag, unless otherwise stated, is on your person. The same goes for your coin purse. It is highly advised you create separate “bags” for your storage at home, coins stashed away, mount equipment, or back up items with your Clone.

## Potions and Consumables

Ensure that whatever consumables you have are updated immediately upon adding them to your inventory or upon their use.

Using the !potion command does not automatically update your inventory. Make sure to remove the consumables you have used from your bag.

## How to Earn Money

Money can be earned by rolling for your daily job in ⁠⁠[💼-job-rolls](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368981723348992) (no RP required, though you may consider making your job roll a part of your character's day to day life for flavour.)

Most money is earned by completing combat encounters and running the **!calc** function. This will take the adjusted XP for the character levels, monster CR, and overall proportional difficulty of the encounter and calculate it into a gold equivalent. Take the **“Adjusted Coin Per PC”** amount**.**

Challenging encounters may reward players with more loot. "Hoards" are difficult encounters that when successfully completed allow players to roll on magic item loot tables. They can sell this loot, or keep it.

## Shopping

Gold must be exchanged for goods and services. Any cheating such as adding items to your sheet without buying or finding them will result in a warning. Repeated and egregious abuse will result in a kick or ban from the server.

### Mundane Items

Basic gear and nonmagical items can be purchased via the **!item** "<item name>" command and purchased for the listed cost in either[🔷-shopping-spam](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368673429291150) or in that specific channel based on what the pinned message instructs you to do. Deduct the appropriate gold and add the item to your bag.

- ALL Poisons/Drugs

## Magic Items

The magic item shops in Nymrathis that are not PC run are refreshed every Sunday. The following shops are currently open:

[⤷🪙artisans-haven](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1240816435660587069)

[⤷🪙callistras-curios](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1240816138070261781)

Magic items are available every week from the Artisans Haven and Callistra's Curios. These are RP channels. All transactions should take place in ⁠⁠the shopping threads within the channels, referencing the pinned prices. The stock quantity is listed per PC and is not a server-wide inventory.  
  
Some PCs may run shops. In that case, the stock is limited by what they have physically collected or crafted. These trades will need to be arranged between PCs.

Some shops are restricted by location access, such as crossing the Komodo Gate between the Obsidian Plaza and the Summit to reach the Arcane Emporium. Keep this in mind when you shop.

There are other shops in the world. You can easily find them by the 🪙in their location name. These shops might be static or also roleplay channels, so make sure to read pinned messages closely to find out.
