## Rarity Limits and Levels

You can only use/equip magic items that are appropriate for your level. We follow close to the guide laid out in the DMG:

- Common - Lvl 1 +
- Uncommon - Lvl 1 +
- Rare - Lvl 5 +
- Very Rare - Lvl 11 +
- Legendary - Lvl 16 +

If you get your hands on something way above your level, you’ll just have to be patient and keep it, or sell it.

## Swapping Attunements

Within the city or the privacy of a stronghold, changing attunements is essentially ‘free’ and there is no limit to the number of changes in attunement you have. In the Wilds or on adventures, attunements require a short rest or long rest to attune to. You are limited to the number of attunements you can change by the number of rests you take. Remember, you can only take 3 short rests per long rest. Choose your attunements wisely.

## Armour and Weapon Enchantments

Magical armour and weapons purchased from the shops are considered “Enchantments” on top of mundane items. This does not apply to items found in a hoard. Magic enchantments to otherwise common items act as "upgrades" and will require one to possess or purchase the base item. For example:

- When you shop for Plate +1, the price listed is the price to enchant pre-owned Plate. If purchasing from scratch, you will have to factor in the cost of the base item.

The same applies to Flametongues and other weapons purchased at the shops. Enchantments cannot stack. You cannot enchant +X variations on Adamantine or Mithral armor. The only exception is Mithral +1 Amor which was published in an adventure and may show up in hoards or shopping availability.

## General Restrictions

### Wearing/Using Items Properly

While items may be flavored to look… almost however the player wishes it, on their sheet, they cannot wear two of the same kind of item as outlined below:

- A magic item meant to be worn must be donned in the intended fashion: boots go on the feet, gloves on the hands, hats and helmets on the head, and rings on the finger. Magic armor must be donned, a shield strapped to the arm, a cloak fastened about the shoulders. A weapon must be held…
- … A character can’t normally wear more than one pair of footwear, one pair of gloves or gauntlets, one pair of bracers, one suit of armor, one item of headwear, and one cloak….
- …Items that come in pairs—such as boots, bracers, gauntlets, and gloves—impart their benefits only if both items of the pair are worn. For example, a character wearing a boot of striding and springing on one foot and a boot of elvenkind on the other foot gains no benefit from either.

### Focus Stacking & Class-Specific Items

Class-specific focus items cannot stack. A character can only benefit from 1 “class-specific item” that increases their spell DC. I.e. Arcane Grimoire, Rod of the Pact Keeper, Amulet of the Devout. If a player is attuned to two foci that increase their class’s spell DC, they can only benefit from the higher item’s DC increase. Multi-classed characters can attune items that increase their other classes’ spell casting but cannot stack on additional bonuses to one class.

To benefit from a magical spell focus, that focus must be held or wielded properly and utilized specifically for the spell being cast. Example:

- If you have both a Dragon Touched Focus and a +2 Grimoire, you cannot benefit from both the grimoire's +2 and the Dragon Touched Focus' teleportation on one spell, as you can only cast a spell through one focus.

### Stat Tomes/Manuals

PCs are limited to benefitting from (1) Tome and (1) Manual. For example: John can benefit from a Con Manual and a Wisdom Tome, but not a Wisdom Tome and an Intelligence Tome.

### Duplicate Magic Items

A character cannot possess and use duplicates of items with charges/per day limits.

- Eg. If you use the Cape of the Mountebank, you cannot swap it out for another duplicate to benefit from it multiple times a day.

The same applies to wands. You may only possess 1 of each type of wand. An equipped companion may also have 1 of each type of wand, or per day limit item, but no duplicates.

Of course, you can always have a spare in your inventory to give to a friend or in case you lose your active one. But you cannot swap them out.

This does not apply to consumable/disposable items.

### Sentient Weapons

If a sentient weapon is found, it requires a ticket and discussion with a Storyteller or Moderator before being attuned or used.

### Elemental Weapons (Dragon’s Wrath, Dragon Wing, Etc)

Should you roll a weapon that comes in varying elemental or damage types you must roll for which type it is. You do NOT get to choose. The additional damage types are as follows:

acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, and thunder
