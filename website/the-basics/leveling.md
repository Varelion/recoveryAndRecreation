## Introduction

All leveling is done through roleplay, with experience awarded per post. You will still gain experience during combat by entering commands, but our main focus, and the way our levelling bot works, is through roleplay. Because of this, there's a few housekeeping notes as it comes to leveling, checking your level, and updating your sheet.

## Accruing XP (Experience)

All leveling is dependent on roleplay and running combat commands. Additional XP may be granted for special events, quests, or hunts but is not guaranteed. The check your progress you can use the **/xp** command in ⁠⁠[🔷-level-up](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368555745509570).

Because of this, please ensure to keep as much OOC chatter as possible out of the roleplay threads. Additionally, avoid any one-liners, gifs, images, emojis and other media in the roleplay threads. XP farming and spam posting is not allowed.

Once you gain a level, you will receive a notification in ⁠[🔷-level-up](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368555745509570).

## Leveling Up

Please ensure when you receive a notification you have leveled up, that you update your character sheet on D&D Beyond, Dice Cloud, GSheets, or whichever other service you use, in a timely manner.

Then, in ⁠⁠[🔷-level-up](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368555745509570) please update your character sheet then use the command !update followed by **!level** and finally **!vsheet**. Upon level up all character get one (1) free long rest, which can be done by using the **!lr** command in ⁠⁠[🔷-level-up](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368555745509570).

If you level up during an adventure you must wait to finish your current combat encounter before leveling up but do not need to return to the city. The only exception to this is if you have “out paced” other party members and cannot adventure together due to a split in tiers. You must return immediately to the city and only once you’ve arrived can you update your sheet and long rest. If you level up in between tiers of a hoard you must finish the entirety of the hoard before leveling up.

## Other Sheet Updates

You can also use **!update** to attune/equip items after adding them on your D&D Beyond Character Sheet (or other sheet service) or to update your prepared spells. Please also use the ⁠⁠[🔷-level-up](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368555745509570) thread for this, along with a short reason (i.e. new items, daily spells) and your updated **!vsheet** or **!sb**.
