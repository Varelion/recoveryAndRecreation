## Introduction

You can be evil, and you can be an asshole. But there are certain rules in play to ensure no player character is taking on the role of a Big Bad Evil Guy, then hiding behind PvP protections.

Plus, Player Versus Player or PvP can be a very difficult area to navigate. Here are some tips and rules to help avoid issues with other players, IC and OOC.

## Player Versus Player

PvP or Player Versus Player is considered any hostile or combative action taken against another player character.

PvP is only permitted with consent of all other players involved in a scene. PvP without consent will result in a warning or a ban. There are many different kinds of PvP, some of which is more complex than others.

## Physical PvP

Physical PvP includes but is not limited to the following:

- Any spellcasting targeting another player (regardless of buff/healing)
- Shoving, grappling, restraining or incapacitating
- Anything involving attack rolls or damage
- Touching another PC beyond a hand shake, tap on the shoulder, or accidentally bumping into them (If you wouldn’t do it to a stranger in real life, you cannot do it on this server without permission first)
- Counter-spelling

All physical PvP must be agreed upon by all players involved OOC first—This includes witnesses or other characters in the area, as some character would want to intervene or leave. All violent physical PvP must be proceeded by pinging a moderator, with the exception of PVP done in the training grounds.

Fights or spellcasting in high traffic public places such as the City Square will be stopped by the guards. If you witness this, ping a Story Teller who will stop the fight. You will be railroaded by automatic save fails.

## Circumstantial PvP

Sometimes circumstance drags other characters into the consequences of your bad actions. Intentionally doing something stupid, jumping the gun with attacking or spellcasting, pulling a ‘Leroy Jenkins’, actively pursuing a destructive course of action, attacking non-hostile or good alignment creatures, drags other players down and is considered PvP. This violates the other player's consent and would be considered PVP as it puts all players at risk.

If you choose to take a risky move, boldly charge in to an encounter, or otherwise “do something stupid” you must inform the other players involved before doing so. This gives them the opportunity to express their concerns or explain their character will try and stop yours or explain their character will leave. If all parties involved do no agree on the plan, or if you do not agree to their PvP reaction to your character’s actions, you cannot proceed with the plan.

The following actions are permitted, though may face consequences from other players. These actions effectively instigating PvP and must be cleared with another player ahead of time:

- Theft
- Serious threats of violence
- Charming, restraining, or otherwise magically influencing another creature.

Players who commit or threaten acts of violence towards non-hostile NPCs, charm them, steal from them or otherwise instigate hostile action must inform other players involved in the scene a head of time. This allows other players to inform whether or not their character would interfere to protect the NPC, and to what level. If you do not agree to the level of PvP reaction from other players, you cannot proceed with your original hostile plan. PvP rules do not protect a character from the consequences of their actions.

## Conversational PvP

PvP is not restricted to physical acts against another PC. PvP may also be verbal, however this server recognizes that heated discussions, and throwing around insults is a part of conversation.

A conversation/interaction cannot start with unsolicited, uncalled for, or otherwise unexpected verbal confrontation.

If a PC gravely insults another character that Player has effectively opened the door to PvP. The receiving character is well within their rights to challenge the other character to a non-lethal duel or other means of ‘defending their honour’. Player characters who have been insulted or otherwise offended, are well within their rights to tell other PCs about what was said to them. This is a part of the territory of verbal/conversational PvP.

You do not need to ask OOC approval before engaging in heated arguments if the conversation has naturally progressed in this manner. However, any player can immediately call a halt to the scene if they are not comfortable or do not wish to continue a verbal altercation. In particularly sticky situations, bringing in a moderator or storyteller or using the [#⛑-safety-discussions](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239341621779108002) channel can help resolve the issue and may handwave a retcon of the conversation if things get out of hand. This does not give a player the right to throw out insults and use safety tools to avoid any kind of retaliation. Abuse of this will result in a warning or ban.

Insults and arguments are a part of conversation. The following, however, are not, and are considered instigating PvP and require OOC permission:

- Shoving, hitting, grappling, or otherwise aggressively touching another character
- Charming, incapacitation, or otherwise magically influencing a PC
- Any action that causes damage to another PC
- Serious threats of violence beyond “…or else”
- Threats of unacceptable behaviour towards NPCs
- Accusations or insinuations of behaviour that was not witnessed or with no evidence of

## Agency

All players are entitled to agency over their character. Other players may not control or assume the actions of another character. During RP you must give other characters a chance to react or respond to your characters actions or words.

## Meta-Knowledge & Forming IC Opinions of Another PC

It's hard on a server where you can read anyone’s RP to avoid imparting metaknowledge on your characters opinions of another. Do you best to keep what you as the player know separate from what your character knows.

That being said, characters are free to form opinions on other characters based off of witnessed behaviour, spoken words, mannerisms, languages used, types of spellcasting used, or anything else that reveals alignment or would reasonably affect a person’s perspective of another. Character are also welcome to freely discuss what they have seen another character do or say and share that with other characters.

Spreading rumours or gossiping without witnessed behaviour is considered PvP at the least, and harassment when done without OOC consent.

Players who feel they are being harassed may open a ticket or ping a moderator and provide specific examples. This does not give a player the right to use safety tools to avoid any kind of retaliation for witness evil or unethical behaviour. Your character’s actions do not exist in a vacuum.

### Reasonable Expectations of Privacy

The following channels are considered high-traffic, public areas:

- The City Square
- Artisans Haven
- Harvest Market
- Riverside Orchards
- Sweet Serenade Cafe (not private rooms or tables)
- Any other place that would reasonably be high traffic. Use your common sense here.

All actions in those places are considered in public view and you have 0 expectations of privacy. You may roll stealth to conceal your actions, but anyone can contest it with Perception. Likewise, many characters may not be actively posting but still present but AFK in these channels. Therefore, privacy of actions is considered largely non-existent on these channels. Take this into account whenever you say or do something in the City Square – It was most likely observed by another character or NPCs.

## PvP and Another Player's Hirelings

Players may create institutions, groups, or following or may have hired staff. These NPCs are under the control of the player and therefore cannot be used or roleplayed by any other player without consent. Labeling creatures in encounters (i.e. assassins, spies, cultists, etc) with another player’s followers/organization/hirelings/etc without OOC permission is infringing on their PC’s agency and is against the rules.

## Villainous PCs

While any alignment is permitted on the server, Villain PCs are not allowed. What defines a villain is vague, and up to interpretation but characters stylized to be Big Bad Evil Guys, or whose entire purpose is centred around evil acts which includes but is not limited to: killing or torturing innocents/non-hostiles, ruling the world, influencing others/NPCs to kill or torture non-hostiles, or eliminating/altering/controlling a populace.

Evil acts are still permitted, but do not exist in a vacuum without consequences. Moderators may not always have the time or headspace to run complicated combat with guards, but that does not mean a player can use PvP rules to get away with evil acts. Committing evil acts or acting villainous, but hiding behind PvP rules reduces PCs to witnesses of atrocities who have no agency to stop them. This is antithetical to the foundations of this server: Consent, respect, and agency. This is especially detrimental to the play experience in a game where players are framed largely as heroes who are adventuring to stop an evil BBEG.

Villains, BBEGs, etc, are exclusively under the control of Moderators and Storytellers. Should your character take a turn down a villainous path, you may engage in a discussion with Moderators or Storytellers about handing over control of that character to them, or turning them into a hoard style encounter. However you will lose control of your character and have to create a new one.

While you may commit evil acts against NPCs, the following actions towards NPCs are considered villainous behaviour and are not permitted:

- Murder of innocents/non-hostile/civilian NPC creatures
- Intentional property destruction of innocents and non-hostile creatures
- Does not apply in **!denc** encounters as that would fall under the Circumstantial PvP rules. Attacking non-hostiles is permitted in encounters, but is not allowed within the city or as RP behaviour.

The following actions are not permitted in any form on the server:

- Torture
- Rape
- Genocide
- Acts of terrorism

Depictions of this behaviour will result in an immediate ban.

## Evil/Monstrous PCs

Due to staff size on this server, we cannot accommodate playing the city guards to enforce laws. However, there’s been some friction with outwardly evil aligned PCs or monstrous PCs (I.e. Undead flavoured, Demonic, Devilish, Vampiric flavoured, etc etc) and the world of Tlalocan. We would like to allow players relatively free reign in the flavour of their PC and alignment to write unique stories. However, it’s important to maintain setting integrity and ensure all players are operating under the same assumptions about what is or isn’t permissible within the city.

Nymrathis is a fantasy medieval/renaissance styled city with people of a wide variety of exposure to the magical and monstrous. The Cobbles is semi-unpoliced, hence the openly evil and fiendish NPCs that appear. The rest of the city is like your standard fantasy town. Monstrous and outwardly evil adventurers are regarded with hostility or at the very least suspicion. Outwardly evil creatures would, if the DMs had more free time, be run out of town or arrested. For now, we are not restricting PCs flavour or alignment, but providing guidance on how to RP your PC in relation to the setting.

The Summit is even more restrictive. Though there are canonically evil NPC nobility, they cover their tracks, use subterfuge and subtly to conduct their diabolical plans. Anything outwardly evil, monstrous, or fiendish would be quickly ‘dealt with’ by the guards of the upper district.

If your PC falls under the “reformed monstrous” or “defying their nature” trope of D&D, they will need to earn the trust of the city and other PCs. Some kinds of monstrous, however, would likely experience the Frankenstein pitchfork treatment no matter how hard they try. They may have to live their lives in the Lower District. It’s up to you how you would like to reconcile the setting with your PC, and we are only writing this to provide more guidance on how the setting would react to your PC. Your PC may need to lie, disguise themselves, or work to prove themselves in the public eye.

**The open worship of the following gods would be met with hostility within the setting of Nymrathis:**

- Bane
- Talona
- Cyric
- Tiamat
- Shar
- Malar
- Lolth
- Loviatar
- Gruumsh
- Kossuth
- Maglubiyet

Along with the worship of any demons, devils, or elder evils. For purposes of law and order, these religions are considered illegal.

**A monstrous PC regarded with hostility unless reconciled in the setting may be of the follow lineages:**

- Undead
- Fiendish
- Aberrant
- Orc
- Hobgoblin
- Goblin
- Kobold
- Bugbear
- Yuan-ti
- Lizardfolk
- Hexblood
- Tiefling

This is not hard and fast, and prejudices and hostility will vary. Tieflings may be regarded with skepticism by more close minded people, but seen as completely fine with most open minded citizens.

Again, we are not making any rules to allow creativity, but providing guidance on the setting. If you would like to be evil and monstrous, you’ll need to do so behind closed doors or in the slums, and create a more palatable public persona. Or, prove your PC can defy their nature/stereotypes about your PCs lineage.
