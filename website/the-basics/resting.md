## Rest Allotments

### Long Rests

Characters can only benefit from 1 Long Rest per IRL day. Long rests take 8 hours of game time. Long rests can be entered in the ⁠⁠[💤-long-rest-spam](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368787065835540) using the **!lr** command.

Characters receive one free Long Rest during level up which can be posted in the ⁠⁠[🔷-level-up](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239368555745509570) thread.

Long rests must be taken within the safety of a city, tavern, or a stronghold. To rest elsewhere, follow the Multi-Day Adventure rules below.

### Short Rests

Characters can only benefit from 3 Short Rests per 1 Long Rest. Use them wisely. Short rests are 1 hour of game time and can be taken anywhere

## Multi-Day Adventures

Players may elect to go on longer "expeditions" to certain areas or choose to long rest in The Wilds. In order to do this the following requirements must be met:

All characters must have the equipment to do so: I.E. Bedroll, tent/tarp, etc.

### Long Resting in the Wilderness

During extended outdoor adventures, players can bypass the 1 long rest per day rule.

Characters can long rest after a minimum of 5 **combat** encounters in The Wilds and 1 encounter in their current location channel. Then, the party may declare they will be resting outside of the city. When a group decides to take a long rest, a single character must make a perception check (DC15 if low danger zone, DC20 if medium danger zone, DC25 if high danger zone) before they go to bed. If they succeed, the long rest is uninterrupted. If they fail, they are interrupted by a denc and must complete said denc before successfully long resting. The party cannot rest again until they complete at least 5 more encounters.

- Eg. The party has completed 4 encounters in The Verdant Expanse and arrived in the Cerro Foothills. They would like to rest. First, they must complete the base encounter in their current location thread. Then, the party decides to go to bed. One of the PCs must make a perception check before bed. Since Cerro Foothills is a low danger zone, the DC is only 15. If they succeed, they can sleep and proceed as normal. If they fail, they must roll a denc and complete it, after which they can sleep and proceed as normal. The party cannot rest again until they complete at least 5 more encounters.

### Exhaustion

If players long rest without eating food, adequate shelter, or adequate heat, they will gain one point of exhaustion and will not benefit from the Long Rest as per the Basic Rules.

### Tiny Huts, Etc.

If using Tiny Hut or other magical shelter (House of Cards) that is virtually impenetrable, players need not roll the perception check for an interrupted rest and their long rest proceeds as normal.

Shelters that provide heat, food, or bedding satisfy the requirements for such.
