## Introduction

We understand there’s a lot to learn, and staff and fellow players are here to help! But there’s a few things to keep in mind when communicating with staff and other players. The main takeaways are the following:

Always try to solve things at the “lowest level”. Other players are very knowledgeable. You likely don’t need a Moderator, Storyteller, or ticket for things such as Avrae commands or “Where is the Market?”

✅ Always consult ⁠[📕-general-rules](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456187872980762788) or [📣-announcements⁠](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456313901103841331) before asking a question. It might have already been answered.

✅ Treat Staff with respect. We are trying to help you, even if we are correcting a mistake, we are trying to make this game fair and fun for YOU. Swearing at, name calling, passive aggression, or hostile behavior towards Moderators or Storytellers helping you, solving problems, making rulings, or correcting mistakes will result in a warning. Repeated or particularly problematic behavior towards the staff will result in a ban or kick.

✅ Respect Staff's time. Do not rush the staff or direct ping a specific staff member in hopes of getting assistance faster because you see them online. The system we have ensures healthy balance and respects the personal time of our staff. A staff member might be online, but they may be taking the day to relax. Our time is volunteer, while we are dedicated to the server it is not the only priority we have.

❌ Never direct message a staff member with server questions unless you have received permission first or unless it is an emergency (i.e. unsafe behavior). Do not direct message staff for opinions on your build unless you have received permission first.

## Questions & Help

The ⁠[❓-questions-and-help](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456129673179172954) channel is to be used for non-time sensitive, straight forward questions or concerns that may be applicable to all or many of the player base. These questions include general DM rulings, server mechanics, spell or item interactions, or Avrae command questions, minor code errors with the Avrae bot.

Please do not ping staff or moderators in this channel as we check it consistently. Please avoid significant irrelevant chatter, jokes, or memes in this channel. It is also primarily a channel for DMs to make rulings.

Particularly complicated questions or questions that pertain to only your character should be taken to a ticket.

Discussing broader changes to rules or mechanics should not be done in ⁠[❓-questions-and-help](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456129673179172954) and should instead be put in ⁠[🤔-discussion-forum.](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1457182641466900572)

Do not ping any moderators directly or the Helper Roles in this channel. We check it consistently.

### Broken Avrae Code!?

It happens all the time. Some is more minor than others. Regardless, do not use the ticket function to report Avrae errors. Just use ⁠[❓-questions-and-help](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456129673179172954) or [🤖-avrae-assistance](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456132329587085534).

## Helper Roles

To help better organize our help system and ensure the right people are here to assist you, this server has a series of pingable roles. You can ping these roles directly in the relevant channel or thread where the issue arises. Pinging these roles means the issue is time-sensitive. Anything that is not time sensitive can go in Questions and help. Despite how time-sensitive the need for help may be, do keep in mind our Moderators and Storytellers are volunteering their time and even if they are online, may not be able to attend to your question right away. Do not demand a ruling be made immediately. Everything on a text-based format can wait.

Please keep in mind, Moderators have the ultimate approval of long-term rulings, but if a Storyteller has taken on one of these roles, they are an authority.

**@Sheet Manager** - Sheet approvals, Retirement, Re-Spec'ing and Build Questions.

**@XP Manager** - For any help with XP or using the XP Bot Scandroid.

**@Rules Expert** - Ruling questions and decisions.

**@Bot Boss** - Anything to do with physical system or back-end. Avrae aliases, snippets, or any errors with the channels themselves/technical aspects of the server. This is not for simple command help, as most likely anyone can help you with this in ⁠[❓-questions-and-help](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456129673179172954) or [🤖-avrae-assistance](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456132329587085534).

**@Monster Manual** - Any questions with regard to monster behaviour, lore, or statblocks. A great resource if you're unsure of how a monster would behave!

**@Volunteer Monster** - Players who love to run combat! These members aren't staff, but you can ping this tag to seek out someone who'd like to run your encounters or hoard rather than a player in your group. Make sure no players present in your party wish to run before pinging this role!

## Ticket Tool

More complicated questions or concerns require a little bit more direct help. That is when one should ⁠[🎫-submit-a-ticket](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456131236228759694). Tickets are used for private or personalized matters that require extensive back and forth with a staff member. Please don't use them to give us a head up about things such as errors in pinned posts, Avrae glitches, or straight forward questions.

### Support Tickets

Support Tickets connect you directly with Moderators. Support tickets should be used for things such as: Mediation for issues between players, concerns with staff behavior, major server issues. We understand that it can be upsetting to have challenges with a staff member, we will remain respectful and professional, so please extend the same courtesy.

### Story Tickets

Story Tickets connect you with the entire staff team. These tickets should be used for quest requests, Stronghold purchases, research requests, and casting any spells that require a DM’s assistance or ruling. Please make these tickets with plenty in advance of taking any action so the staff are not rushed and can provide the proper assistance.

### Character Tickets

Character Tickets connect you with the sheet managers. This is for any re-specs or changes to your character as a result of spells like Reincarnation or True Polymorph. If you open a character ticket to respec your character you are not permitted to adventure or engage in any combat until that ticket has been resolved.

*Tickets transcripts are saved after they’ve been completed to leave a paper trail of all conversation with staff.*

## Safety Discussions

Sometimes things get out of hand in D&D, people run too far with a story. It happens. If something makes you uncomfortable, you feel you are being harassed, or you feel someone has violated the rules/your player agency, you can immediately call for a stop.

When a player calls for a stop, immediately move the discussion to[⁠⁠⛑-safety-discussions](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456128112210214924). If the issue cannot be solved among players in this format, please submit a ticket.

If you feel after the fact that something has had a harmful impact on you or your play experience, please ⁠⁠[🎫-submit-a-ticket](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456131236228759694) immediately. Do not let these feelings fester. Bring them up as soon as you can. The staff exist to make this place supportive and safe.
