## Three Strike Policy

We use tools of moderation to keep gameplay fair and our community healthy. The staff of this server approach moderation from a constructive philosophy. Mistakes and misunderstandings happen, and we would like to catch these before they become bigger, damaging issues.

To better enforce rules and keep our space healthy and happy, the following 3 strike system is used on this server.

**The strikes are as follows:**

1. One Day Mute
2. Three Day Mute
3. Ban

Strikes can be appealed using the [Appeal Form](https://dyno.gg/form/1821dbf0).  
  
**Disclaimer**

Egregious behavior may result in multiple strikes at the same time, or an instant ban or kick. Any content depicting rape, pedophilia, bestiality or OOC violence and harassment towards users will result in an immediate ban and a ban appeal will not be accepted.

## Revocation of DM'ing Privileges

We understand that there is a level of flexibility required to make player-run combat work, and learning new stat blocks at the drop of a hat can be a lot for a player to run monsters. When in doubt, refer to Questions and Help or ping a Monster Manual for assistance. Staff will often check in on combat to ensure the rules and monster behaviour are fair and accurate. These are non-targeted “spot-checks” and we hope to provide quick corrections, not as punishment but to help players learn statblocks, rules, and ensure fair gameplay. Everyone makes mistakes, even our staff!

If, after many corrections, a player still runs encounters incorrectly, unfairly, or maliciously, as a part of the Three Strike Policy they will additionally have their DMing privileges revoked. If you have your DMing privileges revoked, you are barred from running any encounters or arena combat. Violating this will result in a kick from the server. Revocation of DMing privileges will last for three months, after which a player may submit a ticket and discuss with the moderation team about having their privileges returned to them. If the moderation team deems that you have learned the rules well enough, you will be permitted to run encounters with staff supervision for two weeks. After a successful trial period, you may have your DMing privileges returned.
