## Random Rulings

Rulings that don't really fall into any particular category.

### Petrification

Petrified creatures are creatures with the petrified condition and are not objects. They may often behave mechanically as unconscious creatures, such as grapple checks to drag or move. Creatures are not objects unless they are dead.

With regard to spells such as Teleportation, or others that specify they affect “creatures”, a petrified player is considered a creature and not an object.

### Casting Spells or Making Attacks Before Initiative

In combat encounters, you are not permitted to cast spells or make attacks before initiative has been rolled. There are only 2 exceptions:

1. PvP where out of initiative attacks and spells are agreed upon by all players
2. Special circumstances such as personal quests. Some Storytellers may allow the key player in a personal quest to drop a spell/attack before initiative for dramatic effect such as with transformations etc. These circumstances will be made explicitly clear it is a storyteller choice.

This applies as soon as an encounter is rolled. Long duration buff spells such as Mage Armor or PWT are not included in this rule if cast before an encounter is rolled

### Nat 20s and 1s On Skill Checks

Auto success or failures on 20s and 1s with regard to skills checks is only a part of RAW in 3.5E. **It is not a rule in 5E.**

On this server a Nat 20 is not an auto success and a Nat 1 is not an auto failure. (Except for attack rolls as is RAW and some specific environments/traps—these will be detailed in their description and are purview of the Moderators).

In PvP such as contested deception checks, a player may reward another player with additional information on a Nat 20 for flavor but are by no means required or expected to.

Meets it, beats it. Period.

### Underwater Combat

Most underwater dencs we run on the shore with a "no man's land" of space for adventurers and monster. If you're feeling ballsy, you can easily run underwater combat using the [!underwater alias](https://avrae.io/dashboard/workshop/6422d2a55b7f189d31ac90c3).

Please remember the basic rules as per the PHB:

- When making a melee weapon attack, a creature that doesn’t have a swimming speed (either natural or granted by magic) has disadvantage on the attack roll unless the weapon is a dagger, javelin, shortsword, spear, or trident.
- A ranged weapon attack automatically misses a target beyond the weapon’s normal range. Even against a target within normal range, the attack roll has disadvantage unless the weapon is a crossbow, a net, or a weapon that is thrown like a javelin (including a spear, trident, or dart).
- Creatures and objects that are fully immersed in water have resistance to fire damage.

Though the alias should take care of much of this automation.

### Weapons of Certain Death/Chill Touch vs Mythic Actions

Weapons of Certain Death, Chill Touch, and other items that prevent healing/regeneration do not prevent Mythic Actions.

### Guilds and Alt Accounts

In order to ensure no accidental benefiting of alt account characters as well as incentivize the joining of guilds and roleplay with others both, the following rules are in effect:

- Items and gold collected by the guild and its members as a part of guild service, may not be gifted to your alternative accounts if one of your PCs is a part of that guild.
- Your alternative PCs may not benefit from a guild if one of your PCs is a member including status, money, items, room, board, etc.
- Items and gold from a guild's inventory may not be used or given to PCs from outside of the guild, in the exception of a job posted by a guild with the approval of a Storyteller.

### Card Games & Gambling

We have an alias on the server which allows the use of a deck of cards. !deck allows everyone in an initiative count to join a communal card game.

You can play any variation of a card game using a standard 52 set of cards with this alias. Running !deck will give you instructions. You can also cheat by saying you played a card, or shuffling your draw pile, or using magic.

You can play whatever card game you know or can dream of. Just make sure the rules and expectations are clear before starting.

**Example Game - Avernus Hold'em**

Begin initiative with all players joining as if it was combat, then run !deck init as described in the !deck alias.

The player who started the init will be the 'dealer' and their hand will be the Flop, Turn and River. Players will then run through init and draw card in order until each have two cards in their hand. Then, continue the betting rounds as per the Texas Holdem Rules.

Because players can see what's in other people's hands, they must call for an insight check if they notice someone bluffing. The player bluffing then rolls deception. If the bluffer wins, the other players who failed the insight check must operate under the assumption the bluffer has a good hand or they're hard to read.

**PVP**

All gambling or card games can be considered a PVP hot zone. IC interactions may establish the game as fair, and other characters can lie or deceive. However, ground rules must be agreed upon OOC. You must make active insight vs active deception rolls.

**Types of Cheating (Examples)**

Bluffing - Deception vs Insight

Adding a card to your hand - Sleight of Hand vs Perception (must have another deck in your inventory, or a way to magically manifest a card)

- There is no way to add a specific card to your !deck hand but you can RP this. Just remember to describe it when it comes time to resolve

Shuffling your draw pile - Sleight of Hand vs Perception

- "Use !deck reset to completely reset your draw pile to the currently selected deck. In shared card initiative, everyone must discard their cards first, or else it's called cheating!

Magic - Subtle spell/innate spells or Stealth vs Perception (Somatic under table, verbal whispered, etc)

...and anything else you can think of!

OOC you must determine what level of cheating is acceptable. Of course, IC you can lie and say you'll play fair.

**Getting Caught**

It is up to the other players what will happen if you're caught. With a high enough persuasion roll, could you convince them you were just rubbing your nose and not casting a spell?

Do you forfeit all your money?

Do you defend your honor in the arena?

Up to the players!

**Misc**

In the future games may be run by "the house" but currently this is player run. !deck commands, !init and ability checks will be run in the ⁠🃏-games-spam and RP can be done in whatever channel you would like. Though be respectful of others RP and if you're in the Hissing Hag, take it to a private table.

Proficiency with the a Playing Card Set allows you to add your proficiency bonus to any check you use in game including but not limited to insight, stealth, deception, sleight of hand. If you already have proficiency in that skill, you can add your proficiency bonus twice to the check.

### Leaving Your Upgraded Sending Stone Contact

When Sending Stones are upgraded to hold multiple contacts and unlimited charges, they are inscribed with a unique system of runes along the edge similar to a Teleportation Circle's sigil sequence. Traditionally, by tapping stones together they are attuned by magically sharing this sending sigil sequence.

PCs may share Sending Stone contacts without physically tapping their stones together. Instead, one can trace the runes with their finger onto the surface of the stone, granting a connection to the stone whose pattern it belongs to. Thus, this allows PCs to "write their number" on a paper for future attunement, such as with a ⁠[📋-the-board](https://discord.com/channels/1019254075675652157/1077404204840255610) post by writing down the sigil sequence of their Sending Stone.

These sequences can be quite complex, therefore a PC can only write their own runes down and cannot leave the contact of anyone else's Sending Stone other than their own.

### Mirror of Infinite Transpondence - Sending Stone Upgrade

As we move to incorporate Taldorei subclasses, feats, and items, we've decided to make a minor adjustment to the Mirror of Infinite Transpondence to encourage unique RP. RAW, the item is as follows:

**Mirror of Infinite Transpondence**

*Wondrous Item, very rare*

Description

These silver hand mirrors always come in pairs. When activated as an action, a mirror establishes a visual link with its paired mirror for 10 minutes, with the two mirrors acting as opposite sides of the same open window. The mirrors create this connection even if both are on different planes.

Effective Immediately, players can upgrade their Sending Stones at ⁠[🪙callistras-curios](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1240816138070261781) for a luxury sending experience! This upgrades the Sending Stone to allow a similar visual link that appears as an illusory screen projected above the sending stone. The following adjustments have been made:

- In order to maintain the visual link, you must be holding the Sending Stone in one hand pointing in the direction of the scene you wish to project. (i.e. If you put it in your pocket but still hold onto it, it projects the inside of your pocket.)
- If the stone is put away, even for a moment (i.e. to draw or stow a weapon or juggling items in hands), the connection ends and it takes an action to open up the connection again.
- The image projected is as if you are viewing it with your own eyes and with 20/20 vision. There is no zoom function, and the field of view is the same as a normal human's projected onto a rectangular screen.
- The stones may be used to send an "asynchronous" message to another PC, much like the sending stones allow you to leave a "voice mail" to suit the PbP style
- If you and a sending stone contact both have an upgraded stone, there is no need to re-attune stones. So long as both parties have upgraded to the illusory "Infinite Transpondence" they may use the illusory "facetime" function.
- The illusory screen and audio from the illusion can be made either public (i.e. on speaker) or private so only the receiver may see or hear the contents of the communications

Please use ⁠[📤-sending-spam](https://discord.com/channels/1019254075675652157/1028775133604753448) for communications using the "facetime" function, and keep them short (No double posting). Longer communications, such as "livestreaming" a fight, may be placed in the viewer's location channel so long as the public are also involved in the communications. Please don't be that asshole with their phone on speaker in public.

We ask that you do not use private threads for Infinite Transpondence as ⁠[📤-sending-spam](https://discord.com/channels/1019254075675652157/1028775133604753448) allows for us to monitor abuse of items such as revealing one's location in deadly fights for rescue.
