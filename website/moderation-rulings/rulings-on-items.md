### Changes to These Lists

If you spot any items in the shopping or hoard aliases that are on this list, or you otherwise believe they should have been banned but were missed by the staff, please reach out. We would rather catch mistakes before a player starts using a broken item than have to retcon or find a suitable replacement.

A Moderator or Storyteller has the right to refuse the purchase or looting of any item. Appropriate replacement items or GP value will be provided instead. If a moderator sees you buying something or sees an item in a hoard loot roll and has concerns over the item's use or availability for purchase, they will reach out.

These lists is subject to change upon observed or reported abuse.

## Rulings on & Adjustments to Items

### Bag of Beans

You can only benefit from an ASI from the Bag of Beans twice. Be sure to log the ASI increase in 📑dm-references. If you roll 91-99 on the table and defeat the Mummy Lord that spawns, your group may roll **!lootgen** 2 for the reward.

### Bag of Holding (Bomb)

As written in the item description:

Placing a bag of holding inside an extradimensional space created by a Handy Haversack, Portable Hole, or similar item instantly destroys both items and opens a gate to the Astral Plane. The gate originates where one item is placed inside the other. Any creature within 10 feet of the gate is sucked through it to a random Location on the Astral Plane. The gate then closes. The gate is one-way only and can't be reopened.

Bag of Holding Bombs requires a player character to be within 5 feet of the bags, placing one inside the other. You cannot throw bags at a distance or use Mage Hand.

Essentially, if you choose to create a Bag of Holding Bomb, a character will have to sacrifice themselves.

### Broom of Flying or Carpet of Flying vs Prone/Stun/Paralyze/Unconscious

Suffering from the prone, paralyzed, stunned, unconscious condition while riding a Broom of Flying/Carpet of Flying does knock you off the item.

### Dragon Scale Mail and Dragon Essence

You can break down the scale mail into three (3) dragon essence. Hopefully this will help give another use for an often slept on item. You can simply remove the item from your bag in ⁠🔷shopping-spam for ease of logging purposes and then add your essence.

### Magical Melee Martial Weapons & Magical Polearms

Melee Martial Weapons can all be purchased in the same variation as weapons that are listed as (any sword), such as Flame Tongue, Crystal, etc. This allows for magical pole arms and other items. For everything else, open a ticket.

The Polearm Master bonus action to make a melee attack with the opposite end of the weapon **includes** additional damage dice and +x attack and damage bonuses from the magical weapon.

### Mizzium Apparatus Rebalance

When utilizing the Mizzium Apparatus to cast a spell, you are only able to cast spells as if you were only of that spellcasting class and had no other spell slots beyond what it would grant.

For example: A character who is a 2nd-level sorcerer and 16th-level wizard can only use it for up to 8th-level wizard spells and 1st-level sorcerer spells.

### Teleportation Tablet/Conch of Teleportation Destination

If you acquire either of the two items from a hoard, the ruins, or a shop, the default and fixed location they teleport to is the ⁠⛲city-square in Nymrathis. The only exception being if a player crafts a conch with downtime then they can choose a private home or stronghold to be the fixed location of the conch.

### Vestiges of Divergence

Vestiges of Divergence are scaled based on your level. Regardless of what level (e.g. Dormant, Awakened, Exalted) it was found or purchased at, its level will be based off of the attuned PC's level.

**Dormant****:** Level 11-15. You may still attune to this starting level 11 even though it's considered a legendary

**Awakened:** Level  16-19  
**Exalted:** Level 20

The Vestige of Divergence's status is reflective of the PCs level, and if sold or traded to another PC will be adjusted based on their level once attuned. Something something power of the soul.

- Eg. A level 16 PC finds and attunes to a Vestige of Divergence, it is Dormant. If they level up to level 18 it becomes Awakened when attuned. At level 18 they sell the Vestige to a level 20 PC. Once attuned to the Level 20 PC it is now Exalted. If it is then given to a level 16 PC and attuned, it becomes Dormant once again.

### Universality of Spellcasting Foci

We have come to understand the struggles of maintaining one's sheet when having a spellcasting mix. Plus, the Amulet of the Devout already allows this. So...

+X Variants of spellcasting foci may now be used to affect the bonuses/DC of your multi-class spellcasting classes. For example, if you are a wizard/warlock, your +2 Grimoire would also give a +2 bonus to your warlock spells. You must however, still meet the class attunement requirements.

Focus stacking rules are still in effect as they always have been.

We have made this change for a couple of reasons: 1) DDB treats foci this way, and thus it makes it very hard to monitor one's sheet. 2) related to this, it becomes very easy to accidentally stack foci.

### Unarmed Stacking

Due to the addition of multiple items to give monks something decent, it's interfered with our own homebrew rules for things such as Insignia of The Claws.

Items that provide a +1/+2/+3 to unarmed strikes cannot stack. Eg. When the Wraps of Unarmed Prowess are approved, you cannot benefit from both a +X Insignia and a +X Wraps. Treat these items and similar items as you would any +X weapon. They cannot stack. (Due to Eldritch Claw Tattoo's attunement requirement and the fact it's a tattoo, this can stack as we do not have homebrew variations that allow it to be more than a +1)

In Addition: Wyrmreaver Gauntlets can be worn with Wraps of Unarmed Prowess or Insignia of The Claws.

### Wish Items

You cannot craft Wish items (Luck Blade, Ring of Three Wishes, Efreeti Bottle, etc)

**Reminder:** When using a Wish item to perform anything besides replicating an 8th level spell or lower, you still need to roll the 33% failure rate. If you fail, this means you can never cast Wish again, via Items or the Spell itself.

- **Note about Efreeti Bottles**: Since you are not the one casting Wish when using an Efreeti Bottle, there is no failure roll.

If using a Wish Item to perform anything besides replicating an 8th level spell or lower, you are required to open a Story ticket before proceeding.

### Rod of Security

If you use the Rod of Security in the middle of a hoard or encounter, you are considered to have fleed said hoard or encounter, and the hoard will go on cooldown as per usual.

### Nether Scroll of Azumar

This item is now considered a consumable. Upon attempting to read the scroll (regardless of pass or fail on the check), the scroll is consumed and cannot be used again  
  
**Instant Fortress / Mudslick Tower**

These items cannot be used in combat to cause damage to enemies.

**Diamond Spell Gem Addendum**

If you cast Wish into a Diamond Spell gem and give it to someone else to cast, the original caster is as risk of losing Wish if the player using the spell gem does anything besides replicating an 8th level spell or lower.

**You must receive consent from the original caster to risk their Wish.** If you attempt to cast something besides replicating an 8th level spell or lower without the consent of the original caster, this spell will be used and nothing will happen.

### [Item] of Protection Bonus Stacking +3 Max

Protection items (such as Ring of Protection or Cloak of Protection) can only stack up to a maximum of a +3 Bonus!

This means you could have a +1 Cloak of Protection and a +2 Ring of Protection for a total of +3. However a +2 Cloak and Ring would still only total to a +3 max bonus! This is just to keep things from getting too silly. This will operate the same for any item that acts the same as a Ring or Cloak of Protection.

### Living Gloves

When you find and first attune to these gloves, you **must** select one of the options they normally give and they permanently only give expertise in that option. If you find other pairs you can have multiple sets of gloves with different associated tools, and if you currently own any just make sure you declare the type they are going to be—do so in ⁠[📑dm-references](https://discord.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456162218310893722).

### Potions as a Bonus Action

Drinking a potion (this includes **Keogthom's Ointment** but **not** **Enchanted Brew**) or administering it to another creature **requires a Bonus Action**. Applying an oil might take longer as specified in its description. Once used, a potion takes effect immediately, and it is used up. **You cannot drink or administer a potion with your action.**

### Dragon Essence

Dragon Essence (DE) is earned by, you guessed it, killing dragons! You do not need to get the "final hit" to earn Dragon Essence, simply participating in the combat is enough to collect power from a dragon's fading soul.

- Wyrmling - 1 DE
- Young - 1 DE
- Adult - 2 DE
- Ancient - 3 DE

Additionally, the ⁠Lair of the ████████ grants players 5 DE for defeating the Ancient Dragon and their minions.

Now, what counts as a Dragon for this? Well, pretty much anything with the Dragon type. Assume anything that falls outside your standard Chromatic, Metallic, and Gem dragons is only worth 1 DE (Exceptions to this are creatures like the Dragon Turtle, Moonstone Dragon, and Sea Dragon, which follow the normal DE amounts based on age). Additionally, creatures like the Ghost Dragon and Dracohydra do not give Dragon Essence. Keep in mind that killing Metallic dragons is an innately evil act unless in self defense. Likewise, many Gem dragons are Neutral, so make sure you have a good reason to pick a fight! You can mark your usage of DE in ⁠[📑dm-references](https://discord.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456162218310893722), and although you do not need to wait for approval, be sure to ping moderators when you do.

**Upgrading Rarity**

The required Dragon Essence that must be put into an item to upgrade its rarity.

- Wakened ➜ Ascendant - 50 DE
- Stirring ➜ Wakened - 30 DE
- Slumbering ➜ Stirring- 10 DE

This is paid for each step, i.e.: to go from an Slumbering all the way to a Ascendant would require 90 DE in total. D

**Changing Element/Dragon Type (****Random** **Ro****ll)** **- 10 DE**

To reroll the element of a dragon item (acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder) or to change the dragon type (Metallic, Gem, Chromatic).

**Changing Element/Dragon Type (Choice, but Permanent) - 25 DE**

To choose the element of a dragon item (acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, poison, psychic, radiant, or thunder) or to change the dragon type (Metallic, Gem, Chromatic). This choice is permanent and you cannot change the type again at a later point.

**Purifying Dragon Mask - 50 DE**

To purify a Dragon Mask in the name of Bahamut, God of Justice and Nobility, The Platinum Dragon. This allows the mask to be worn publicly and by Good aligned characters without threat of Tiamat's influence. The masks change color based on their previous element:

- Red ➜ Gold or Brass
- Blue ➜ Bronze
- Black ➜ Copper
- Green ➜ Steel
- White ➜ Silver

You cannot purify a Dragon Mask using DE collected from Metallic Dragons.

- When using Dragon Essence to modify an item, up to two other players can also add their Dragon Essence to the item.
- Dragon Essence modified items are **not soul bound** for full clarity.
- Every 1 Dragon Essence is worth a maximum of 200 GP. This is the purchasable limit (similar to the GP limit on Tlalocanum trading).
- You cannot "trade Dragon Essence" and give it to other players via their cc.

### Familiar Hiding In Bag Meta

- Action - Normal Actions
- Bonus Action - Normal BAs
- Free Object Interaction - Open bag
- Movement - Half Movement to enter/exit bag

This operates on the assumption **the bag closes on its own** after the Familiar goes back inside. This is the currently how Familiars are played; we are simply defining how the Action Economy works.

**Stab Those Bags**

Hello Monsters, have you had this problem? You keep downing a pesky adventurer but their silly magic pet in a bag keeps feeding them potions! Well have I got a new strategy for you: stab those bags!

But seriously, if an enemy **has reason to stab/destroy a bag**, we have rules for that now! Keep in mind this only works with **targeted attack** since most worn items are protected from AOEs. See the below list of bags with AC, HP, Resistance, and Damage Immunities! We've even added some fun Homebrew bags to add to the strategy.

**You can search for all these items (even the Homebrew versions) with !item [name] and use !bagstats to bring up the entire list with their HP, AC, Resistances, and Immunities.**

- Can hold one cubic foot or 30 pounds of gear.
- 1 Tiny Creature
