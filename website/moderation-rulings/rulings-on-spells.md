## Introduction

Some spells and abilities have been changed to suit the server's constraints and to maintain balance. Newer rulings not added here may be in ⁠[📣-announcements](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456313901103841331).

## Casting Year-Long Spells

In order for some spells to become permanent, they require year long casting. Due to the practical restraints of casting the same spell for a year, your character can choose to 'Purchase' the effect of this spell to create a permanent effect. (i.e. Teleportation circle)

To do this, a gold value is calculated using the following formula, based off the Adventure League 'Purchasing Services' options:

**C = Consumable Component Cost**

**M = Non-Consumable Component Cost**

**L = Spell Level**

**= 52*((L*L) + (C*2) + (M*0.1))**

If tied to a location, ensure you drop this calculation, along with the spell being casted in the location thread. If being one on property you do not own, then you will need to get permission from the owner, or the staff (in the case of a public area).

## Spells Requiring Consultation With a Moderator

Some spells, either their effects or the actual mechanics of their execution, work differently on this server due to the settings and play format.

As a general rule, any spell that stipulates "as determined by the GM/DM" or "the DM/GM decides" requires the player to contact a Moderator or Story teller via a ticket prior to casting the spell. This ensures no abuse of powerful spells and a fair application of them. Do not cast the spell and then request a DM, you will be told your spell fails and the spell slot and materials are consumed. Many of these spells require significant preparation and lore on the part of the DM and it is unfair and disrespectful of their time to assume they can immediately respond to your use of the spell.

The following rulings have already applied to these spells:

### Divine Intervention

*“If you roll a number equal to or lower than your Cleric level, your deity intervenes. The DM chooses the Nature of the intervention; the Effect of any Cleric spell or Cleric domain spell would be appropriate. If your deity intervenes, you can't use this feature again for 7 days.”*

In order to use this feature you will need to submit a ticket so a DM can determine the appropriate intervention. For the most part, Divine Intervention will work similar to Wish.

### Wish

Should you have the opportunity to cast Wish for any purpose **other than producing the effect of an 8th level spell**, you must contact a DM via ticket. **Additionally, Wish cannot revive anyone.** This applies to Revivify, Raise Dead, Resurrection, and Reincarnate, Clone.

Wish requires the player character to be extremely clear and specific about their wish. The DM may interpret this in any way. Keep in mind, Wish sometimes has adverse effects. A particularly disruptive or unrealistic wish will cause the spell to fail and the spell slot to be consumed.

You cannot stack resistances from Wish. If your character has gained resistance from Wish, they cannot gain another. In addition, using wish to give resistance to a damage type can only target two creatures rather than ten.

Once the specifics of the wish are discussed over a ticket, the player may not know the exact outcome of their wish until it happens. Once a wish has been cast, there is no taking it back. The wish must be expressed in a single sentence that can reasonably be spoken within an action (6 seconds).

### Divination Spells

Divination requires a DM to act as the answering authority on any spell such as Divination, Legend Lore, or Commune.

Divination spells that clarify a ‘question’ is asked, the question must be phrased within 1 sentence that can reasonably be spoken within an action (6 seconds).

The same rules apply to Speak With Dead and similar spells.

### Gate

A moderator must be contacted when attempting to use Gate. Only a Storyteller or Moderator can run NPCs on the other side of the ‘Gate’. You may not start to cast this spell without an open ticket to avoid putting DMs on the spot for something that is quite big.

### Planar Binding

**You** **must make a story ticket and inquire about the creature before attempting Planar Binding. If you are casting it mid-fight you must ping @Moderator.**

There are some limitations on which creatures we allow to be planar bound.

- The creature's CR can't be higher than the PC's Level-5
- The creature may not have Legendary Actions or Legendary Resistances

Staff reserves the right to reject the binding of certain creatures or adjust the binding times as needed if they deem them to be unbalanced.

## Spell Alterations and Rulings on Spells

Some spells have controversial rulings, others just don't "work" in this setting.

The following rulings on spells have already been made:

### Find Familiar

The expanded familiar list includes: Tressym, Abyssal Chicken, Almiraj, Flying Monkey, Fox, Hare

### Teleportation Circle

If you learn the Teleportation Circle spell the two sigils sequences you learn are: The one located in ⁠[⤷📚ivory-library](https://discord.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456326222232682576) and another one that makes sense for your character and will be one of the following: the temple, school, academy, or bardic college they studied at. You may hand-wave a visit back home or RP teleporting back to said location as a part of a quest.

### Teleportation

You may use the Teleport spell and similar teleportation mechanisms normally within Nymrathis. Other cities in Tlalocan will require either a home in said city to teleport to (keep in mind that certain cities have specific requirements to purchase a home in them) or for you to fulfill certain conditions (e.g. Completing the trials in Nerii grants access to the Teleportation Circle). You may Teleport from The Wilds back to Nymrathis but you cannot Teleport out into The Wilds. Some locations are completely barred from teleportation out and will say as such in the pinned posts.

### Planar Ally

The CR of a creature summoned by Planar Ally may not exceed your level. You cannot summon any creatures with Legendary Actions or Legendary Resistances. It cannot be a ‘one of a kind’ creature either. Once you are able to cast such a spell, you may open a ticket and the DMs can create a list of acceptable summons for you to call on. Additionally, payment will be a minimum of:

- 500 gp * CR per encounter
- 10, 000 gp per day for non-combat tasks.

Additional payments or requests may be incurred in the event of particularly dangerous or challenging tasks.

### Simulacrum

Your simulacrum cannot adventure without its creator. If its creator dies, a simulacrum cannot then ‘become’ the PC that made it.

### Shadow Blade

Shadow Blade can be used together with Booming Blade and Green Flame Blade. Though the material components in the strictly technical sense don't line up, the creator expressed that was not the intent when reworking the cantrip.

The melee weapon created by Shadow Blade must be one that, if not made of shadow, would be worth 1sp.

### Infinite Summons

Creatures created by summoning or other spells such as Animate Dead, etc cannot summon other creatures. Simulacrums cannot cast Simulacrum. No infinite hax.

### Casting Spells Before a Rest

A short rest is 1 hour and a long rest is 8 hours. If you cast a spell with an 8-hour duration before a long rest, that spell is no longer in effect after a long rest. You do not get to pre-cast spells, and long rest to get the slots back. You may Extend a spell with sorcery points, or cast spells with longer durations. But keep this in mind for spells like Aid.

While in-game time is a little loopy, the system of 5E is balanced so that you must spend resources to gain the benefits. You cannot buff, then rest to recover spell slots, and head out with those buffs still intact unless they last longer than the long or short rests you have taken.

### Invisibility/Blindsight and See Invisibility

Invisibility only grants an advantage if the attacker is unseen. If a target can see invisibility or has senses such as Blindsight, True Sight, etc, they do not still get an advantage.

### True Polymorph, Clone, and Simulacrum

Many wizard players have raised questions about True Polymorph and Simulacrum. The following adjustments have been made to avoid abuse of the spells or game-breaking nonsense:

- Both Clone and Simulacrum will use the statistics, creature type (in the case of Clone), appearance, race, and abilities of the un-polymorphed version of the target, regardless of whether the spell was cast while true polymorphed.
- Simulacrum may be used on Fey, Monstrosity, Ooze, and Construct PCs. But ONLY PCs.

If your character would like to engage in a fantasy gender transition, we highly recommend opening a ticket with staff to do so. We would love to help your character find a permanent way to do this! Likewise, if you wish for this to be something already written previously into your character's backstory, feel free to do so.

### Horizon Walker and Planescape Spells

Effective immediately, Horizon Walker Rangers gain additional spells as a part of the Horizon Walker Magic feature on this server:

- 5th Level: Warp Sense
- 13th Level: Gate Seal

These spell count as a ranger spell for you, but it doesn’t count against the number of ranger spells you know.

### True Resurrection and Hoards

The bodies of PCs who fall in hoards and are not brought back to the city are considered "destroyed" for the purpose of using True Resurrection.

### Silvery Barbs

This is now a 2nd level spell. Make sure you are casting it using **-l 2** or higher.

### Goodberry

Goodberries can be eaten as a bonus action, though it still takes a full action to feed one to someone else.

## Entering AOEs

AOEs that have an area that requires characters to enter it such as Sickening Radiance, Spirit Guardians, Moonbeam, etc, very clearly state that saves and the damage from such only take place:

- "When a creature enters the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there..."

Sage Advice further explains that the save and the damage taken as a result from it only takes place on the target creature's turn. Not on the casters. You cannot drag creatures into an AOE and have the effect take place on your turn. The save will only take place on the creature's turn if they start their turn in the AOE or enter the AOE on their turn.

Sage Advice clearly states:

- “Our design intent for such spells is this: a creature enters the area of effect when the creature passes into it. Creating the area of effect on the creature or moving it onto the creature doesn’t count. If the creature is still in the area at the start of its turn, it is subjected to the area’s effect.”

## Unconsciousness and Willingness

Unconscious or incapacitated creatures are considered unable to give consent and therefore cannot be willing or unwilling creatures.

For the purposes of spellcasting and spell effects, this server will operate using the concept of implied consent for spells such as teleportation that rely on willing targets.

If that same creature in their right mind would consent to the spell, especially if it has harmless effects (I.e. teleporting to safety), the unconscious target is considered willing. That being said, OOC consent is a requirement.

This does not apply for unconscious enemies, harmful or malicious applications, or anyone who would, if conscious, would not consent to the spell. As a general rule of thumb, the implied consent rule can only be applied in life or death situations. If you need to really think about whether or not an unconscious character would be willing in their right mind, they are probably unwilling.
