## Introduction

Some feats and abilities have been changed to suit the server's constraints and to maintain balance. Newer rulings not added here may be in ⁠[📘-rulings-and-approved-homebrew](https://discord.com/channels/1019254075675652157/1024744337017426032).

## Banned Subclasses

For the sake of server balance, some things have to be banned.

- Bard: College of Tragedy
- Cleric: Moon Domain

## Rulings on Class Abilities

### Artificer

Due to ignoring level requirements, an artificer may equip legendary items upon reaching **level 14 Artificer** as opposed to the 16 required by the server

Due to ignoring spell requirements, an artificer with **Magic Item Savant** can use a Manual of Iron Golems before having two 5th level spell slots

Due to ignoring spell requirements, an artificer with **Magic Item Savant** can use spell gems and spell scrolls without making the spellcasting check normally required for the spell level being higher than what they can normally cast

### Sword Bard

***Defensive Flourish.*** *You can expend one use of your Bardic Inspiration to cause the weapon to deal extra damage to the target you hit. The damage equals the number you roll on the Bardic Inspiration die. You also add the number rolled to your AC until the start of your next turn.*

Defensive Flourish only adds the amount rolled by the bardic inspiration die to your AC, not the damage dealt. However Avrae is unable to make this distinction for crits and vulnerabilities. Essentially granting Swords Bards +20 to their AC.

Do not add -dtype or crit arguments to your defensive flourish ability so the AC can be properly calculated. This will deal a flat, generic "weapon" damage. Should you wish to use a damage type the creature is vulnerable to or if you crit, you will need to adjust the hp manually to reflect this.

### Echo Knight

**Flying Echoes**

Echoes do not "fall" from the sky if they end their turn in the air.

**Unleash Echo with Dread Ambusher or Haste**

You cannot unleash Echoes on Haste, Dread Ambusher attacks, or similar ‘extra’ attack actions that are not a proper Attack.

### Shadow Sorcerer

**Hound of Ill Omen**

The Hound is now able to wear items as per the companion rules. When re-summoned, those items are still on it, however, the items on the Hound aren't accessible to the player when the Hound isn't summoned. To outfit it outside of combat, one must spend their sorcery points and summon the Hound.

### Hexblade Warlock

**Accursed Specter**

The specter can equip magic items as per the equipped companion rules. Once it dies/you take your long rest and fades away, you'll need to spend time to re-equip your next Specter.

### Soul Knife

**Psionic Blades and Opportunity Attacks**

Soulknife Rogues can “draw” a psychic blade for the purposes of Attacks of Opportunity

### Warlock of the Great Old One

**Create Thrall**

You cannot make a thrall out of a creature that has Legendary Resistances or Legendary Actions.

### Oathbreaker

**Control Undead**

You cannot control an undead that has Legendary Resistances or Legendary Actions.

### Bloodhunter

You may meet the Bloodhunter multiclass requirements using wisdom in lieu of intelligence so long as that stat is being used for your class modifier.

### Thief Rogue x Alchemist Artificer

We allow the Thief Rogue feature Fast Hands to work with the Alchemist Artificer Experimental Elixir. Basically: you can use your cunning action to use elixirs. You're welcome.

### Blood based abilities

For all features (think specific blood hunter, blood cleric, blood wizard features, etc.) that state they only work if the target has blood, they now work on all creatures that are **not undead or constructs**. This is to keep things less confusing and more consistent.

## Rulings on Feats

### Polearm Master

**Pikes**

Polearm Master bonus action can be performed with a pike.

### Dragonlance Backgrounds and Bonus Feats

Dragonlance backgrounds are unique to the setting. While you may choose it to open up the prerequisite for certain feats you do not gain bonus feats at level 4. Additionally the first level bonus feat counts as your first level free feat, such as with Spelljammer backgrounds.

### Flash Recall

Similar to our Mizzium Ruling, you must have the spell slots for that level from that class's spellcasting progression to prepare the spell.

### Cartomancer

The Cartomancer does NOT give a free spell slot. In order to imbue this card you must expend the spell slot. Note, the imbuing happens when you finish a long rest, so you cannot create the Hidden Ace, then long rest, and cast Wish twice.

Additionally, much like our Mizzium ruling, When utilizing the Hidden Ace feature, you are only able to imbue spells to the spell level in that class you have achieved. I.e. If you are a Level 2 Cleric and a Level 10 Wizard, you can only imbue Cleric spells of the 1st level.

## Rulings on Racial Abilities

### Dhampir Bite

For features such as the battle smith ability that lets you use intelligence instead instead of attacking with strength or dexterity, the monk feature that allows you to use dexterity instead of strength, or the ability to choose a Hexblade Hex weapon to use Charisma instead of dexterity or strength, we’re allowing the constitution modifier of the Dhampir bite to be replaced by those abilities if the other requirements are met.

### Warforged (Variant)

We are allowing Warforged characters to change their creature type to Construct.

Now what does this mean? It comes with positives and negatives...

- You are unaffected by any spells that say "Humanoid". No more Hold Person on you!
- You are also unaffected by most healing spells, such as cure wounds, healing word, mass cure wounds, mass healing word, and spare the dying.
- Weapons or spells that deal extra damage or apply Disadvantage to saves for Constructs affect you!
- Mostly you need to keep track of yourself, but you can also change your type with a command !manage type set Construct
