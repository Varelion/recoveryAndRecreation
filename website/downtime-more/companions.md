## Introduction

This section is all about Companions! There are a lot of different terms involved, so this section is about defining the different types of Companions and their individual rules.

Companions does not include hired staff or similar creatures that do not adventure or perform active functions (such as house keepers, unseen servants, etc).

### Definitions

- **Companion**: Any creature traveling with and controlled by a Player Character. This includes summons, simulacrums, golems, familiars, and mounts.
- **Class Companion**: A Companion granted via a Player Character's class feature (e.g., Ranger's Beast Companion, Drakewarden Drake, Artificer’s Steel Defender). Class Companions do not count against the one (1) **Equipped Companion** limit.
- **Equipped Companion**: A single Companion chosen by the Player to use magic items or gear for which it has proficiency. Players may only have one (1) Equipped Companion at a time.
- **Mount**: Any creature ridden by a Player Character. Mounts can use barding or magic items they qualify for and do not count against the one (1) **Equipped Companion** limit, provided they use only mount-specific or passive-effect items.
- **Minions:** A Minion is anything that doesn’t fall under Class Companion, Equipped Companion, or Mount. A Minion's **maximum CR** can be up to your proficiency bonus halved, rounding down. So an example, my character is level 6 with a +3 proficiency bonus, that means a minion could be a creature with a CR of 1 or less. Whereas my level 17 character with a +6 proficiency bonus could have a minion that is CR 3 or less. Other minion rules still apply this is just to help clarify what can or cannot be classified as a minion. Examples: creatures like Skeletons and Zombies used by a Necromancer, or unequipped Summons/Creations like Find Familiar that last more than 1 hour. Minions may only use items listed in their stat blocks, +1/+2 variations of those items, or items that grant passive effects (e.g. Periapt of Wound Closure), provided it meets the item's requirements. If a minion is equipped with **any other items**, it becomes your one (1) **Equipped Companion**.

### General Rules and Restrictions

1. **No Legendary Companions**
  - Companions with Legendary Actions or Legendary Resistances are not allowed.
2. **Taming Creatures**
  - Wild creatures cannot be tamed as Companions or Mounts unless granted by a class feature.
  - Players wishing to acquire a creature Companion outside of class features must submit a ticket for a custom quest.
3. **Death Saves**
  - Companions and Mounts make death saving throws at 0 HP, except for:
    - Temporary summons or non-permanent creatures.
    - Creatures explicitly stated to disappear or die at 0 HP (e.g., Find Familiar, Simulacrum).
    - Minions.
4. **Party Size**
  - These are the Companions that count against your Party Size when creating your adventuring team:
    - Simulacrum
    - Golems
    - Mounts that cast spells or take the attack actions, **not including** Class Companion Mounts.
    - Any Minions beyond your first five (see Minions section in Equipping Rules for more details).
5. **Initiative**
  - You may group your minions and companions with yourself in combat, though their turns are to be taken directly after yours unless the wording for said minion/companion specifies it acts on your turn, e.g. Simulacrum acting during your turn in combat. Mounts can take their turn on your turn as long as you use them as a controlled mount with limited actions - see DMG rules on controlled mounts for reference.

### Equipping Rules

1. **Standard Equipment Restrictions**
  - Items that grant new attacks, spells, or non-passive abilities are prohibited.
  - Familiars (e.g., those summoned by Find Familiar) cannot equip or use items that grant damage or attack capabilities, as per the spell’s limitations.
  - Class Companions/Familiars which allow you to cast a spell through them are **not** considered spell casters for the purposes of attunement.
  - Skeletons are permitted to wear light or medium armor.
  - The Artificer’s Spell-Storing Item may be given to Player Characters or Class Companions that require a bonus action to command.
  - You may group your Companions with yourself in combat, though their turns are to be taken directly after yours unless the wording for said Companion specifies it acts on your turn, I.E.: Simulacrum acting during your turn in combat.
2. **Equipped Companions**
  - Players may designate one (1) Equipped Companion to use items outside its stat block, provided it meets the item's requirements.
  - Switching which Companion is the Equipped Companion can only occur outside of combat and no more than once per Long Rest.
  - Equipped Companions cannot equip items of a rarity higher than **Rare**.
3. **Class Companions**
  - Class Companions can use items outside its stat block, provided it meets the item's requirements.
  - Class Companions do not count against the one (1) **Equipped Companion** limit.
  - Class Companions cannot equip items of a rarity higher than **Rare**, unless they are being used as a **Mount**, in which case they cannot equip items of a rarity higher than **Very Rare.**
4. **Mounts**
  - Mounts may equip barding, mount-specific items, or magic items that grant passive effects (e.g., Periapt of Wound Closure), provided it meets the item's requirements.
  - Mounts are assumed to be proficient in all Barding.
  - Mounts do not count towards party size so long as they do not cast spells or take the attack actions.
  - Mounts do not count toward the one (1) **Equipped Companion** limit **unless** the PC dismounts and actively uses the Mount in combat. The exceptions to this rule is if the PC becomes unconscious, and the Mount fights to protect or save the PC, or if the Mount is also a **Class Companion**.
  - Mounts cannot equip items of a rarity higher than **Very Rare**. The only exception to this limit are the **Legendary Mount items** sold at the [#🔒🐲drakewarden-garrison](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456327752243478590).
5. **Minions**
  - Minions may only use items listed in their stat blocks, +1/+2 variations of those items, or items that grant passive effects (e.g. Periapt of Wound Closure). If a Minion is equipped with any other items, it becomes your one (1) **Equipped Companion**.
  - You are limited to 10 Minions in total. 5 of those Minions you can bring with you for free. For every Minion after 5, they each take up 1 space in your Party Size.
  - Minions cannot equip items of a rarity higher than **Rare**.

## Tupperbox Reminder!

The use of Tupperbox is permitted, however you may only use it to RP your own characters. Anyone using a tupper alias to RP another character or an NPC that does not belong to them will be banned.

Ensure your tupper alias is properly named to avoid any confusion between what is a PC and what is a DM controlled NPC. Please set up your Tupper name in the same format as your username:

- Character Name [Level] Username

Additionally, if you are using a Tupper for your hirelings or minions you must indicate to whom the hireling belongs in their tupper name. Eg,

- John, Joe’s Hireling

If you are using a Tupper for narration, please indicate it is a PC narrating by adding [PC] at the end. Eg,

- Narrator [PC]

DMs will use Tuppers that do not have this formatting and will simply be the NPCs name or “Narrator” or “DM”. Using this formatting allows other players and moderators to see who is typing and avoid any miscommunication between PCs as DM NPCs follow a different set of rules.

Please avoid using Tupperbox during combat commands. It can get messy.
