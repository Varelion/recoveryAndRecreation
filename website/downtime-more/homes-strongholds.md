## Homes

Homes are available for purchase in the The Cobbles, Obsidian Plaza, The Summit, and the Steading Lands via [City Homes](https://discordapp.com/channels/1449914978927644807/1456133865813839923). Once a home is purchased, a player can buy expansions for their home to tailor it to the play experience. All homes include furniture.

**Homes have a limited footprint and can only be expanded a certain number of times.**

Due to the sheer size of Strongholds, they can be set up as channels in the "Strongholds" category. Strongholds can either be created as private or public channels, but are considered private scenes either way. You may make threads, or even private threads within the channel, but the main content will be visible to all approved players. Rules that apply to Public/Main Location channels will also apply to your Stronghold (i.e. ERP will be restricted to appropriate private or bedroom threads. ERP will not be allowed in the main channel or threads intended for public use i.e. Workshops, Stables, etc, unless it is made explicitly clear in the title that channel is NSFW.).

Small strongholds or ones that you intend to use more like a standard house thread, can be set up as either a public or private thread. If you would like your stronghold to be open to the public to visit, such as a trading post, it can be added as a stop listed on the main location channel's pinned post so others can visit. In order for someone to visit your stronghold without you, they will need express permission to do so either OOC or IC. Attacking or breaking into strongholds will follow the PVP rules. You can handwave that your stronghold, i.e. trading post, is open for anyone to visit by adding it to the pinned post or consulting a moderator to add it to the main location channel's information. If your thread is open for everyone to access, you and your visitors cannot use it for explicit roleplay and should instead consider making it a channel with explicit sub-threads.

## ❗Maintenance Fees❗

All Strongholds will have a hand-waved 'Staff Quarters' for any hirelings you deem as 'live-in staff' and can fit as many staff and hirelings as given as default under "Maintenance Fees". Living quarters and food for additional staff beyond that can be purchased as an expansion. The base maintenance fee covers all living expenses including living quarters and food for the listed number of hirelings.

**Maintenance fees must be paid every 3 Months.**  
**Maintenance Fees for strongholds in the wilds are double!**

Failure to pay Maintenance Fees will cause your staff to abandon you and your stronghold to deteriorate. You have 1 month to catch up on payments or you lose your stronghold.

Additional hirelings can be hired separately as per the hireling rules. All additional hirelings must be supported with a place to stay and food (See Staff Quarters and Farm expansions).

**Additional Maintenance Fees for Strongholds Outside of The City**

Lack of immediate access to supplies, exposure to the elements and enemies means that all Maintenance fees are doubled for any location placed in The Wilds.

Placing a stronghold outside the city allows you to use it as a base of operations to avoid interrupted long rests on multi-day adventures. However it does not negate encounters upon going to bed or waking up. These encounters will take place and RP'd as curious creatures attacking, investigating, or visiting your stronghold and will be run in the main channel for that Stronghold. The environment for the encounter will be determined based off of the location it is placed. Similar to long resting and travel rules you must do the following:

- Roll for an encounter on arrival (Including if you teleport to this location)
- Roll for an encounter before long resting (PM encounter)
- Roll for an encounter immediately after long resting (AM encounter)

You will need to leave your stronghold to complete these encounters and cannot simply hide inside and 'wait it out'.

### Teleporting to A Stronghold in The Wilds

You may teleport to your stronghold and can also start your adventure from this location!

## Keeping Track of What's In Your Stronghold

In the pinned post of your stronghold, you will need to keep track of your paid maintenance fees, expansions, siege weapons, hirelings. Please use the following format:

- Stronghold Type: Tower, lodge, etc
- Features: (# of Bedrooms, expansions, teleportation circles, permanent spells etc)
- Remaining Unused Expansion Slots: #
- Hirelings: # of skilled, # of unskilled, # of guards
- Maintenance Costs Paid Up To: Date
- Additional Hirelings Paid Up To: Date

Homes with hirelings, extra features, or expansions should use a similar format.

## Running a Shop or Trading Post

If you choose to build a shop or trading post, it must be manned by either you or a hireling. You can offer services that your character is trained in such as spells, tool proficiencies (item forgery, cooking a meal), crafting, etc or a hireling can act as a shopkeep to sell items you have earned from a hoard or crafted yourself or any harvested materials you have found. You cannot buy items from the market and resell them in your shop. You cannot sell items created by Fabricate. Please list what is for sale or what services you offer in the pinned post.

- Name of Shop | Owner's Name
- - Item Offered, Price
- - Item Offered, Price
- - Service Offered, Price

This is not passive income. Another PC that is not one of your alts must be the one to purchase the services or items. Services provided using a tool proficiency such as food or beverages such as with your Cook's tools, Brewers tools or document forgery will be pre-approved at the creation of your shop, though can be updated or expanded upon with consultation with a Moderator or Storyteller.

The downtime and/or materials required per instance of the service provided will be established ahead of time and listed in the pinned post of your shop. Likely, your services will involve a skill check based off your tool proficiency and a downtime cost dependent on the level of service you wish to provide but will largely follow what is outlined RAW in XGE. Offering anything outside of what's been pre-approved will require consultation with a Moderator or Storyteller.

Selling spell services or feature services (such as Chef's feat treats) will cost spell slots or uses of that ability. This is not free income without any resource sink. You may offer services that are completely RP-based with no mechanical benefit to customers, but that must be made clear prior to purchase.

## Transferring Ownership of A Stronghold

Should you wish to retire your character or your character dies and you have declare a beneficiary of your Stronghold, you may transfer that ownership over to another PC which cannot be you alt.

This new character will have to pay a property tax equal to the total cost amount of the stronghold, including expansions, enchantments and take over all maintenance fee requirements. Please submit a ticket in order to transfer your stronghold.

Note: Property tax does not apply to standard homes but payment of any hirelings must be taken over by the new owner.

You must declare a beneficiary IC before your character dies or retires. Otherwise the home or stronghold will be foreclosed.

## Selling Property

Selling a home incurs some loss, unfortunately. To sell your property, transactions can be left in the housing district threads, or in a ticket for strongholds. Property can be sold for the base value. I.e. a middle district home sells for 5,000 gp, but you do not get gold back for your expansions.

To upgrade a stronghold, you will need to sell and then purchase a new property (though this may be RP’d as construction on the same site). Selling a stronghold must be done in a ticket. Do not add or deduct gold in relation to selling or buying your stronghold outside of a ticket and before approval from staff.

### Property Limits

A player character is limited to one stronghold ever, and 1 home in each city

## Hirelings

Hiring staff to take care of your home, or staff to man your temple or business costs money. We go by the PHB/DMG rules for hirelings. You will pay for the hirelings every 3 Months.

3 Month Cost per Skilled Hireling - 730 GP

3 Month Cost per Unskilled Hireling - 72 GP

With the exception of Stronghold Siege Battles (Under Development), hirelings cannot fight, cast spells, or earn money on your behalf and are for RP purposes and general tasks and maintenance. The only exception may be in the case of specific DM-run quests.

Hirelings will leave you if abused or mistreated or if they are not paid. Hirelings cannot leave Nymrathis or your Stronghold unless discussed as a part of a story or quest.

## Hireling Statblocks

In the future, hirelings may take place in Siege Battles (Under Development). They can only fight in these battles and cannot be taken on adventures or otherwise benefit you beyond RP.

Skilled Hirelings may use any of the following statblocks:

- Acolyte
- Guard
- Bandit
- Cultist
- Scout
- Soldier
- Tribal Warrior
- Apprentice Wizard

You can flavour these as any race, or use statblocks for intelligent Humanoids of a CR less than 1 such as...

- Goblin
- Bullywug
- Gnoll
- Kobold
- Lizardfolk
- Orc
- Hobgoblin
- Kenku

When you purchase your Stronghold or hire the hirelings, you must state how many are of each type and list it in your pinned post. You may swap out the statblocks of your hirelings every 3 months when you pay your maintenance fees.

Unskilled Hirelings use the Commoner statblock and cannot use Siege Equipment.
