## Harvesting/Skinning From Creatures

Harvesting creature parts isn't only fun, it's required for crafting! Please note you cannot sell any items you harvest from creatures at the market because the alias isn't balanced with this server's economy.

Skinning creatures is exclusively for RP and crafting. The gold calculation at the end of combat covers things such as looting corpses of equipment, money, etc.

After killing a creature, you may attempt to harvest various items from it. Run the !skin <creature> command to attempt to harvest parts of an animal. Keep in mind each creature can only be harvested a certain number of times in total depending on the size.

!skin Attempts by Creature Size

- Tiny - 1
- Small - 2
- Medium - 3
- Large - 4
- Huge - 5
- Gargantuan - 6

Each individual player can only collect one material from a fallen monster. You can skin for your friends and hand them the part, but one character cannot go home with everything from a fallen monster.

A failed attempt destroys the part attempted to be skinned and no other players can attempt to harvest it.

Items collected via the **!skin** command cannot be sold at the Market, but can be sold to other player characters. This is simply because the alias is not balanced to this economy and messes with our loot system.

## Tracking Creatures

If you are hunting a specific creature, and would like to search for them in a specific area you may attempt to track a creature down, or use Locate Creature to find it. Once you successfully find this creature, you only encounter one (1) of them. A PC can track a maximum of 3 different creatures per real-time day.

If you locate or track a creature, then leave the location channel (including sub-threads) you lose track of the monster. The creature must be on the server. You will know if this creature exists by having already encountered it or by asking a mod. Otherwise a ticket is required.

### Tracking via Non-Magical Means:

After completing an encounter at a location channel, you may roll a Survival (Wisdom) check. The DC to find is 10 + CR. The following requirements must also be met:

- You are in a location that has the proper environment for that creature. A location's environment is determined by which !dencs you can roll there. A creatures environment is listed in the tags on the D&D Beyond listing for the creature.
- If the creature has no environment, you must ask a moderator first.
- There are no abilities on the creatures stat block that make it untraceable by non-magical means.
- You have not attempted to track this creature within the last 24 hours.

A successful survival check means you have found tracks or other indications of the creature's passage. Then, you may follow them. You will have to roll one (1) additional encounter for the region prior to 'finding' the creature.

### Locate Creature

You may attempt to use locate creature to find a specific creature, however keep in mind the rules of the spell:

The spell can locate a specific creature known to you, or the nearest creature of a specific kind (such as a human or a unicorn), so long as you have seen such a creature up close - within 30 feet - at least once. If the creature you described or named is in a different form, such as being under the Effects of a Polymorph spell, this spell doesn't locate the creature.

This spell can't locate a creature if running water at least 10 feet wide blocks a direct path between you and the creature.

Upon casting Locate Creature, the following requirements must be met for the spell to successfully track the creature:

- You are in a location that has the proper environment for that creature. A location's environment is determined by which !dencs you can roll there. A creatures environment is listed in the tags on the D&D Beyond listing for the creature.
- If the creature has no environment, you must ask a moderator first.
- There are no abilities on the creatures stat block that make it untraceable by magical means.
- You have not attempted to track this creature within the last 24 hours.

It is unlikely a creature will be both within 1000 ft and not behind more than 10ft of running water, even more so if it is a rare creature. Therefore, the spell has a percentage chance of success the equals 20-CR, with CRs below 1 equaling 0.

- I.e. You use Locate Creature to find a Phoenix. A phoenix's CR is 16. Therefore the chance of locating a Phoenix is 4%.
- Note creatures CR 20 and above cannot be found using Locate Creature.

Roll 1d100. If the result is equal to or less than the chance of success, you successfully locate that creature.

Then you will roll for one (1) encounter for that region before you may begin an encounter with that creature.

### Scrying

You cannot use scrying to locate a vague type of creature, i.e. "I am looking for a red dragon in the desert." - This would not work as it is not a particular creature.

Additionally, this spell would not work if someone has told you of a monster's existence in a certain region, as it may have died or been killed within that time fame.

For these reasons, scrying cannot be used to find random creatures. Use of scrying against specific creatures (NPCs, etc) is allowed, but will require you to open a Story Ticket.
