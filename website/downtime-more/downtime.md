## Introduction

When you set up your character, please make sure to use the **!timer** on function to begin accruing downtime. We use this alias for Downtime.[https://avrae.io/dashboard/workshop/625627897cd69bb4dfa75db8](https://avrae.io/dashboard/workshop/625627897cd69bb4dfa75db8)

However, due to burden on DMs from this alias, use of downtime required moderator approval. The only exception is copying spells, writing a spell scroll, or learning a skill.

To remove downtime, use **!timer -<# of days>** in Timer Spam. Additionally help can be pulled up with **!timer** help.

## Copying A Spell

In order to copy spells, please follow the official rules for copying spells. To copy a spell:

- When you find a wizard spell of 1st level or higher, you can add it to your spellbook if it is of a spell level you can prepare and if you can spare the time to decipher and copy it.

- Copying that spell into your spellbook involves reproducing the basic form of the spell, then deciphering the unique system of notation used by the wizard who wrote it. You must practice the spell until you understand the sounds or gestures required, then transcribe it into your spellbook using your own notation.

- For each level of the spell, the process takes 2 hours and costs 50 gp. The cost represents material components you expend as you experiment with the spell to master it, as well as the fine inks you need to record it. Once you have spent this time and money, you can prepare the spell just like your other spells.

- If copying from a scroll, roll an arcana check with a DC = 10 + Spell Level. On a failed check, the copying fails and the scroll is destroyed.

## Learning a Skill, Language, or Tool

You can use your downtime to learn a skill at the ⁠[⤷📚ivory-library](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1240817328375988264). You can spend downtime to earn proficiency or expertise in a skill using the following guideline:

### Any skill takes 10 weeks to learn. Tools and languages take 5 weeks to learn.

-1 week if tutored by someone proficient in skill, tool, or language.

Plus...

### Languages

-1 week for every intelligence modifier point.

### Skills/Tools

-1 week for every ability score modifier point related to the skill or tool, listed below:

- Alchemist's supplies - Intelligence
- Brewer's supplies - Constitution
- Calligrapher's supplies - Dexterity
- Carpenter's tools - Strength
- Cartographer's tools - Intelligence
- Cobbler's tools - Dexterity
- Cook's utensils - Constitution
- Glassblower's tools - Dexterity
- Jeweler's tools - Dexterity
- Leatherworker's tools - Dexterity
- Mason's tools - Strength
- Painter's supplies - Dexterity
- Potter's tools - Dexterity
- Smith's tools - Strength
- Tinker's tools - Dexterity
- Weaver's tools - Dexterity
- Woodcarver's tools - Dexterity
- Disguise kit - Charisma
- Forgery kit - Charisma
- Herbalism kit - Wisdom
- Navigator's tools - Wisdom
- Poisoner's kit - Intelligence
- Thieves' tools - Dexterity
- Any Musical Instrument - Charisma
- Any Gaming Set - Charisma

You can set up your tool proficiencies using the **!tool** alias. Get help using **!tool** help

Regardless of deductions listed above, the minimum time required is still **3 weeks** for skills, and **1 week** for Tools/Languages.

Tuition at the school is 100 gp per week of study. It also gives access to the library for players to research.

Expertise in a skill or tool can be learned using the exact same format above. You must first have proficiency in a tool or skill to gain expertise.

## Research

This server uses the !research alias to use 1 day of downtime per research question. Research is performed at the [⤷📚ivory-library](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1240817328375988264). You must open a story ticket if you would like to research and MUST inform the storytelling staff what you are trying to research before running the alias. **Do not go to the library, run the alias, and ping a storyteller.** This puts our staff on the spot. Instead, open a story ticket, ensure you have a storyteller assigned to your research, then run the command there.

The only exception to this is if you are researching something that is standard 5e lore that you as a player know the answer to, but your character does not. You may run the research alias yourself, without seeking out a moderator, so your character may gain knowledge of a specific topic and avoid any issues with meta knowledge. The topic you research must be expressed within a single sentence and be relatively specific.

- I.e. You cannot ask “What are all the gods in this setting and all of their domains and tenets.” It must be able to be answered within one to two paragraphs.
