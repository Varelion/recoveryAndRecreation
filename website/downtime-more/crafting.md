## Crafting Spell Scrolls and Healing Potions

Unlike the other crafting rules, you do not need materials harvested from monsters to create spell scrolls and Healing Potions, but must provide the gold cost component, unless you have an expansion in your home. Use ⁠[🔨-crafting-requests](https://discord.com/channels/1239236397672697987/1239367972074553364) to deduct downtime and gold cost, and write a note of what you are crafting and any abilities/expansions that are reducing the time or cost.

## Crafting Magic Items/Potions

Crafting any magic item or potion other than the exceptions above requires approval in Crafting Requests. Under no circumstances are you to remove gold or materials from your bag to craft an item without approval from the staff.

This server will determine crafting via a precedence-informed case by case system. Rulings will be based off the official guidelines for crafting as laid out in WOTC material including Xanthar's Guide to Everything. Some modifications will be made to accommodate unique server requirements.

To submit a request for crafting start a post in ⁠⁠[🔨-crafting-requests](https://discord.com/channels/1019254075675652157/1040312399506452480) with details of what you want to craft, the materials you think would work, and your relevant tool and skill proficiencies. A moderator will then work with you to determine appropriate gold/downtime/additional requirements.

**You must have relevant tool or skill proficiencies in order to craft or enchant.**

You cannot “take” downtime from other PCs. They may assist you crafting something and you can use their downtime for a portion of it, however if they are assisting you they must have relevant tool or skill proficiencies. You may receive help from up to 2 other people, and they cannot spend more downtime on the item than your character is.

- I.e.: Joe asks Sue and Beth to help him craft a Rare Item. Sue offers 5 days, Beth offers 10, and Joe must spend 15.

Players that wish to craft a homebrew item must specify the stats of that item and should expect a lot of back and forth to create a balanced item.

For magic items you will require a material harvested in order to craft. It should be vaguely relevant to the item being made (i.e. Dragon's heart for a flame tongue) and be harvested from a creature in an appropriate CR range as listed in XGE.

## Expansions that Assist with Crafting

**Larger Garden** - Provides herbs suitable required for crafting potions and other similar items. Eliminates gp requirement for Common and Uncommon potions and reduces the gp requirement by half for rare and very rare.

**Laboratory** - Provides all the space and tools required to craft acid, alchemist's fire, antitoxin, oil, perfume, soap, potions, and poisons. Reduces the cost to create these items by 1/2. Reduces crafting time on Rare and above consumables by 1/2.

**Workshop** - Provides all the space and tools required to craft armour, weapons, tools, and other similar items. Eliminates GP requirement for crafting common and uncommon items. Reduces crafting cost by 1/2 for Rare and above. It likewise reduces crafting time by...

- Rare - 14 days
- Very Rare - 21 days
- Legendary - 85 days
