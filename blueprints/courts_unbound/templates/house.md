# 🏛️ House & Bloodline Submission Guide

**Houses** are families and orders with secret arts. **Bloodlines** are powers of birth. Submit either here, and wait for **Approved**, before anyone plays it. Rules: {#abilities}.

- Describe it fully: history, traits, and what its power does as it grows.
- No member limit. You may ask members to consult you, but you may not refuse anyone whose history fits.
- House arts are taught only by house members. Bloodlines are inherited.
- Staff weigh balance against existing abilities.
