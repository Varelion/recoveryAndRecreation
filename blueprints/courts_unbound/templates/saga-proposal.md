# 🌀 Saga Proposal Template

Rules: {#sagas-guide}. Open a new post here with this template and tag it **Proposed**. Staff and Storytellers answer in your post.

## Copy below
```
**SAGA:** name
**PROPOSED BY:** @you (character)
**PREMISE:** what happens, and why now
**STAKES:** what changes if each side wins
**SIDES:** who fights whom, and who you need
**DE:** yes / no (if no, what the danger is instead)
**WHERE:** locations, and which become branches
**ZONES:** skirmish branches for Tiers 1-2? a main battle?
**LENGTH:** expected real-time length
**PRIZES:** optional
```
