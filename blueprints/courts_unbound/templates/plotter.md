# 🗺️ Plotter Template

A plot page helps partners find you. One per character, titled with the character's name. Tag **Open** while you want new threads.

## Copy below
```
**CHARACTER:** name · profile link
**WANTS:** what they want most right now
**FEARS:** what they will not face
**CONNECTIONS:** who they know, players and NPCs
**LOOKING FOR:** rivals, mentors, missions, romance (state your romance role)
**THREADS:** links to current and past threads
```
