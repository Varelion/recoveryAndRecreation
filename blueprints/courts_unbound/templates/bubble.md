# 🫧 How Bubbles Work

Bubbles are scenes that fit no location forum: a private home, a long journey, somewhere between places.
- Title them `Location | Scene name`.
- Tag **Open** or **Closed**, and **DE** if death-enabled.
- A bubble still happens somewhere: that location's danger rules apply.
- Bubbles idle for 14 days are tagged **Finished**.
- Explicit scenes never belong here. See {#after-dark}.
