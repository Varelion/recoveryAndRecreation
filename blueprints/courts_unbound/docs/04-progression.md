# 📈 04 · Progression

Abilities grow the way skills do: through practice, study, battle and story. Progress is counted in **qualifying threads**, recorded by staff when a thread is graded in {#thread-inbox}, and claimed by you in your {#upgrade-ledger}.

## Qualifying threads
A finished thread qualifies when it meaningfully uses or develops an ability:
- **Training:** study, drills, sparring with a purpose. "My rival beat me again, so I build a counter" qualifies. Punching a dummy a hundred times does not.
- **Battle:** a real fight where the ability mattered.
- **Story:** threads with weight: missions, sagas, court intrigue, consequences.

Each graded thread credits **one** ability; a saga thread credits **two**. Chatting over tea credits nothing, however long it runs.

## Costs
Raising an ability from one tier to the next takes as many threads as the new tier's number:
- Beginner → Novice: 2 threads
- Novice → Intermediate: 3
- Intermediate → Adept: 4
- Adept → High Adept: 5
- High Adept → Substantial Mastery: 6
- Substantial Mastery → Mastery: 7
- Mastery → Grand Mastery: 8

**New abilities** cost **3 threads for each ability you already hold** and must happen **on-screen**. Awakening a dormant bloodline counts as unlocking. Holding six or more abilities adds one thread to every advancement: spread thin, grow slowly.

## Limits
- **5 solo training threads** per character per week count toward progress (weeks reset Sunday 00:00 UTC).
- **2** of them may be **boosted**.
- No ability may exceed your rank's **cap** (see {#ranks}): Tier 1 Adept, Tier 2 High Adept, Tier 3 Mastery, Tiers 4 and 5 Grand Mastery.
- In-character time runs at twice real time. Nobody masters a bloodline in a season.

## Boosts
Market items and properties multiply a training thread's credit (see {#market-guide}).
- Declare the boost in the thread's **first post**. Undeclared boosts do not count.
- One boost per thread. Boosts never stack.
- Examples: Illyrian weights ×1.25 to ×1.75; a training circle ×1.75 or ×2; training at your own residence ×1.1 to ×1.5.
- Partial credit adds up: two ×1.5 threads equal three threads.

## Secret and forbidden arts
- **House arts** must be taught by a member of the house.
- **Court secret arts** need the ruler's leave, unless you are Inner Circle or the ruler.
- **Forbidden magic** needs an approved special claim and a completed quest (see {#claims-guide}).
- All three are unlocked and advanced **on-screen** only.

## The Upgrade Ledger
Every approved character keeps one post in {#upgrade-ledger}. Use the pinned templates:
- **RANK UP:** an ability's new tier, with the threads that paid for it.
- **I HAVE LEARNED:** a new ability, with the on-screen thread where it happened.
- **PROFILE ADJUSTMENT:** changes to appearance, personality or history.

A {@Lorekeeper} confirms each entry. Then update your profile.

## Enforcement
This is mostly an honor system. If you advance faster than your threads support:
1. A grace warning, and help finding your footing.
2. Repeat offences: the advancement is voided.
3. Deliberate abuse: progress frozen for a week.
4. Beyond that: progress frozen for a month.
