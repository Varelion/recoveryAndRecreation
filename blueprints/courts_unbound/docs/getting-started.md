# 📍 Getting Started

Welcome to **Courts Unbound**, a 21+ alternate-universe roleplay set in the world of A Court of Thorns and Roses. The courts of Prythian are real. The people are yours. No canon character exists here.

## 1 · Read
- {#rules}: the rules. Staff rulings are final.
- {#setting}: what happened, and what is happening now.
- {#courts}: the seven courts, the courtless and the outlaws.

## 2 · Apply
This server is **21+**, and Discord accounts must be **six months** old. Apply at <https://acotar.us/apply/>: your birthday, a few answers in your own words and a short roleplay sample. A younger account needs a member with {@Approved Player} to vouch for you with `/vouch`, or a censored ID shown to staff in your application thread. Once staff accept you, you become a {@Member}. Until then you hold {@New Arrival}: you can read, and chat in {#waiting-room} and {#general}.

## 3 · Pick your roles
Ping, romance and spectator roles are in {#roles}.

## 4 · Make a character
- Read {#creation}, then {#abilities} and {#ranks}.
- Want to start above Sworn, or use a reserved power? Read {#claims-guide} and claim **before** you write.
- Once accepted, open a post in {#character-profiles} with the pinned template. Tag it **WIP**, then **Pending Review** when it is done.

## 5 · Get approved
A {@Lorekeeper} reviews your post. Once approved you receive {@Approved Player}, your allegiance role and your tier role.

## 6 · Play
- Open your ledger in {#upgrade-ledger} and your purse in {#market}.
- Find partners in {#looking-for-rp}, join a mission on {#mission-board} or a saga on {#saga-board}.
- Finished a thread? Post it in {#thread-inbox} for Renown, Marks and progress.

-# Only here to read? Take {@Spectator} in {#roles}.
