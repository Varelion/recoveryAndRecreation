# 🗺️ 07 · Missions

Missions are focused stories: a task, a danger, a reward. They are the most reliable way to earn Renown and Marks, and good missions make good stories.

## Tiers
- **Errand:** no real danger. Escorting a letter, guarding a harvest fair, finding a lost hound. 1 Renown, 4 posts each.
- **Task:** a risk of trouble. Bandits on the road, a smuggler to question. 2 Renown, 8 posts each.
- **Venture:** expect a fight. Clearing a naga nest, stealing a ledger from a rival court. 4 Renown, 15 posts each.
- **Peril:** serious danger, enemy fae and real stakes. Raiding a Hybern spy-house. 6 Renown, 21 posts each. May turn DE if the plot demands it.
- **Nightmare:** combat is certain and death is likely. Hunting a Dread outlaw, entering Under the Mountain. 10 Renown, 28 posts each. **Always DE.**

Post counts are per person. Marks come from grading as for any thread (see {#renown}).

## Who may take what
- **Tier 1** (Sworn, Wanderer): Errand, Task.
- **Tier 2** (Courtier, C and B-Class): up to Venture.
- **Tier 3** (Emissary, A-Class): all. Nightmare needs a companion.
- **Shadows, Tier 4 and 5:** all, alone if they wish.

A lower-tier character may join a higher mission led by someone who could take it alone, and is DE like everyone else.

## Who may run them
- **Any Courtier or higher** may run Errand and Task missions for other players.
- **{@Storyteller}s** run Venture, Peril and Nightmare missions.
- **A High Lord or Lady** may issue court missions up to Venture to their own court, without a Storyteller.
- The game master voices NPCs and the world, never another player's character.
- A game master's own posts are graded like any group thread.

## How it works
1. The game master fills the template pinned in {#mission-board} and sends it for a staff check: Storytellers in {#story-workshop}, everyone else in a **Mission Review** ticket ({#submit-a-ticket}).
2. Staff post the approved mission in {#mission-board}, tagged with its tier and **Open**.
3. Players reply inside the mission to join. The game master updates the roster and tags it **Full**.
4. The mission is played as a post in a location forum, or in {#rp-bubbles}, tagged **Mission**.
5. When it ends, the game master tags it **Done** and posts it in {#thread-inbox}.

## Honest difficulty
A mission must be as hard as its tier. If staff find a Peril that was really a Task, they grade it as a Task.

## Court missions
Sworn characters can ask for work in their court's hall. Rulers, regents and the Inner Circle hand out court missions there, and the court remembers who answered.
