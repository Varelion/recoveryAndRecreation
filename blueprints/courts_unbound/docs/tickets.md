# 🎫 Tickets

Open a private ticket with the panel below, or at <https://acotar.us/tickets/>. Pick the closest type:
- **Character Review:** questions about your application or ledger.
- **Claims:** rank, special or relic claim questions.
- **Mission Review:** a mission you want to run.
- **Dispute:** a fight ruling, a DE appeal, a disagreement between players.
- **Report:** a rule breach. Include links and screenshots.
- **News:** something your character did that deserves the {#court-gazette}.

To join the server, apply at <https://acotar.us/apply/> instead.

Never DM staff about server business; tickets keep a record and reach every staff member.
