# 📗 09 · Character Creation

Writing is the core of this server. There are no stat blocks: a character is a story, a personality and a short list of abilities.

## 1 · Know the world
Courts Unbound is an alternate universe. No canon character exists, and no canon event after the Treaty happened. Read {#setting}, {#courts} and {#rules} first. Your history must fit a Prythian twenty years after the War of Thorns.

## 2 · Choose your people
- **High Fae:** the default.
- **Lesser faerie:** pick a kind (naga, kelpie, pixie, river-spirit, something new) and one natural trait.
- **Illyrian:** usually Night Court born, war-camp raised. Wings fly only as a bloodline (see {#abilities}).
- **Peregryn:** usually Day Court born. Wings fly only as a bloodline (see {#abilities}).
- **Human:** from the Mortal Lands. Short-lived, magicless, resourceful.
- **Made:** claim only (see {#claims-guide}).

Traits are listed in {#abilities}.

## 3 · Choose your allegiance
One of the seven courts, **Courtless** or **Outlaw** (see {#courts}). Humans are usually courtless; a human sworn to a court is rare, and interesting.

## 4 · Choose your rank
Everyone may start as **Sworn** (court), **Wanderer** (courtless) or **Petty** outlaw. Anything higher is a rank claim: claim it in {#claims} first.

## 5 · Choose your abilities
Your rank gives your slots and tiers (see {#abilities}).
- A **bloodline** costs two slots, dormant and awakened. A fresh Sworn spends both slots to start with an awakened bloodline.
- A **house** art costs one slot. Custom houses and bloodlines go to {#houses} for approval first.
- Not on any list? Invent it, and staff will judge it for balance with the rest of your application.
- Forbidden magic, relics and the Court Power are never starting abilities (see {#claims-guide}).

## 6 · Write it up
Open a post in {#character-profiles} titled **Last, First**. Copy the pinned template. Tag your allegiance and **WIP**. Personality is the most important section; history matters next.

Then answer **In your own words**, about you rather than your character: what collaborating in good faith, IC/OOC separation and this alternate universe mean to you. Not definitions; 40 words or more each.

## 7 · Submit
Switch the tag to **Pending Review** and ping nobody: a {@Lorekeeper} will find it. Staff may adjust formatting and set your server nickname to your character's name.

## 8 · After approval
- Open your ledger in {#upgrade-ledger} and your purse in {#market}. Every character starts with **500 Marks**.
- Write a plotter in {#plotters} if you want partners to find you.

## Character limits
- Two characters are free.
- Each further character needs **30 in-character posts** on your newest one.
- Inactive characters may be archived after a staff warning.
