# 🌟 05 · Renown, Bounty and Marks

Three numbers, kept by staff in {#ledger-log}:
- **Renown:** how famous you are, and how your court treats you.
- **Bounty:** how badly the courts want an outlaw dead.
- **Marks:** money, spent in the {#market}.

None of them make you stronger. A powerful character can be unknown; a weak one can be famous.

## Renown thresholds
- **Courtier, C-Class:** 10
- **B-Class:** 15
- **Emissary, A-Class:** 25
- **House Head:** 30
- **Inner Circle:** 50
- **High Lord / Lady:** 50, granted on ascension

Reaching a threshold makes you **eligible**, nothing more. Characters claimed at a higher rank start with that rank's Renown. A dead character's Renown is gone with them.

## Earning Renown
Missions pay by tier, per person (minimum posts per person in brackets):
- **Errand:** 1 (4 posts)
- **Task:** 2 (8 posts)
- **Venture:** 4 (15 posts)
- **Peril:** 6 (21 posts)
- **Nightmare:** 10 (28 posts)

Fame fades for easy work:
- At **10+** Renown, Errands pay nothing.
- At **10+**, Tasks pay only in a group. At **25+**, they pay nothing.
- At **25+**, a solo Venture pays 1.
- At **50+**, only Nightmare missions, captures, kills, events and sagas pay.

Staff may also award small amounts for anything genuinely famous, or take Renown away for anything infamous: fleeing a fight you swore to fight, betraying an ally, being publicly humiliated.

## Hunting and being hunted
**Capturing or killing an outlaw** pays twice their class's mission value:
- Petty 2 · Notable 4 · Dangerous 8 · Infamous 12 · Dread 20

**Outlaws earn Bounty** by killing people of note:
- Sworn or Wanderer 3 · Courtier 5 · Emissary or Shadow 10 · Inner Circle 15 · High Lord or Lady 25

Outlaws who kill outlaws earn nothing. Outlaws gain Bounty from crimes and missions just as others gain Renown.

## Marks
Marks come from **graded threads**. For each character:

**Marks = graded words ÷ 10 × quality × thread type**

- **Quality:** 0.5 for careless writing, 1 for solid, 1.5 for excellent. Spelling, clarity, voice and interest count.
- **Thread type:** solo ×1 · group ×1.25 · saga ×1.5 (active participants only; spectators get group rate).
- **Caps:** at most 2,000 graded words per post, and 20,000 solo words per character per week.

Businesses can raise what you earn (see {#market-guide}). Example: a 6,000-word group thread written well pays 6,000 ÷ 10 × 1 × 1.25 = **750 Marks**.

## Getting graded
Post finished threads in {#thread-inbox} with the template there. One post per thread; if your last request has not been graded yet, edit it instead of posting again. Staff record Renown, Bounty, Marks and progress credit in {#ledger-log}.
