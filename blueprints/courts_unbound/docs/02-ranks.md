# 👑 02 · Ranks

Rank is standing, not power. It decides which missions you may take, where you may go and whom you may command. Each rank has a minimum **Renown** ({#renown}), but Renown alone never promotes anyone: your court promotes you, in play.

Ranks group into five **tiers** ({@TIER 1} to {@TIER 5}). Your tier sets your mission access and your **ability cap**, the highest proficiency any of your abilities may reach (see {#progression}).

## Starting ranks
- Anyone may start as **Sworn** (court) or **Wanderer** (courtless).
- Any higher starting rank is a **rank claim**. See {#claims-guide}.

## Court ladder
### Tier 1 · Sworn
Newly oath-bound. Errands for the court, training, little trust.
- **Slots at creation:** 2 (Novice, Beginner).
- **Missions:** Errand, Task.
- **Limits:** cannot lead teams or missions; leaves court lands only with a Courtier or higher, or a writ.
- **Ability cap:** Adept.

### Tier 2 · Courtier · 10 Renown
Trusted members of the court. Courtiers teach, lead small parties and carry the court's business abroad.
- **Slots at creation:** 3 (Adept, Intermediate, Novice).
- **Missions:** up to Venture.
- **Command:** up to three Sworn. May lead Errand and Task missions.
- **Travel:** may leave court lands on official business.
- **Promotion:** the seasonal **Court Trials**, a public test held in each court.
- **Ability cap:** High Adept.

### Tier 3 · Emissary · 25 Renown
The court's elite: its captains, envoys and spymasters, trusted to act in the ruler's name.
- **Slots at creation:** 4 (Substantial Mastery, High Adept, Adept, Intermediate).
- **Missions:** all. A Nightmare mission needs at least one companion.
- **Command:** up to three Sworn or Courtiers. May lead any mission.
- **Travel:** free, for personal or official reasons.
- **Promotion:** a private trial set by the ruler.
- **Ability cap:** Mastery.

### Tier 3 · Shadow
Hand-picked by the ruler. Shadows assassinate, spy and vanish. Rank, age and record do not matter; the ruler's trust does.
- Same slots and cap as an Emissary.
- **Missions:** all, including Nightmare missions alone.
- **Secrecy:** only the ruler and the Inner Circle know a Shadow's face. Revealing yourself while masked means expulsion and worse.
- **Arts:** may learn the court's secret arts with the ruler's leave.

### Tier 4 · Inner Circle · 50 Renown
At most **three** per court, appointed by the High Lord or Lady (or by the regent while the throne is empty).
- **Missions:** any, alone.
- **Arts:** may learn every secret art of the court without permission.
- **Travel:** free, without leave.
- **Ability cap:** Grand Mastery.

### Tier 5 · High Lord / High Lady
One per court. The court's power chose them, and the court must live with it.
- **Claimable at launch:** see {#claims-guide}.
- **Slots:** as an Emissary, plus the **Court Power** slot (see {#abilities}).
- **Authority:** final word on the court's decisions; assigns missions, promotes, appoints the Inner Circle, issues writs of passage.
- **Permanence:** after **3 months** of play the title is permanent. A ruler who steps down remains "the former High Lord" for life.
- **Ability cap:** Grand Mastery.

## Courtless ladder
Reputation classes, earned with Renown.
- **Wanderer (Tier 1):** as Sworn, without court training.
- **C-Class (Tier 2) · 10 Renown:** as a Courtier.
- **B-Class (Tier 2) · 15 Renown:** 3 slots, with a primary of up to High Adept.
- **A-Class (Tier 3) · 25 Renown:** as an Emissary.

## Outlaw bounty classes
An outlaw's former rank is stripped. They are ranked by **Bounty** ({#renown}) instead, and every court keeps their name.
- **Petty (Tier 1):** 5 Bounty or less.
- **Notable (Tier 2):** 6 Bounty.
- **Dangerous (Tier 2):** 15 Bounty.
- **Infamous (Tier 3):** 25 Bounty.
- **Dread (Tier 4):** 40 Bounty. Dread outlaws are a threat to all Prythian; every court shares what it knows of them.

An outlaw keeps the slots they had. Recognized outlaws are always death-enabled (see {#combat}).

## Titles
Titles stack with rank.
- **{@House Head}** · 30 Renown: leads a house (see {#abilities}).
- **{@Master}:** owns an Academy and teaches a signature art (see {#market-guide}).
- **Lord or Lady of a Keep:** owns a Keep and rules its holding (see {#market-guide}).
- **{@Shard-bearer}:** holds a Cauldron Shard (see {#sagas-guide}).
