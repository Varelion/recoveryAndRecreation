# 📌 08 · Claims

Some things are limited: high ranks, faces, forbidden magic, relics. You claim them in the registers in {#claims} before you build a character around them. Do not design a character on the assumption that a claim will succeed.

## Rank claims
Start a **new** character above Sworn or Wanderer. Existing characters are never promoted by claim.

**Open at launch, per court:**
- 3 Courtiers
- 2 Emissaries
- 1 **High Lord or Lady**

**Open at launch, courtless:** 4 C-Class, 3 B-Class, 2 A-Class. Courtless claims may also be outlaws, at the matching bounty class.

**How to claim:** reply in the **Rank Claims** register with the position you want and a roleplay sample of at least **300 words**, or **500 words** for a throne. Staff judge grammar, vocabulary, voice, attitude and activity. The higher the rank, the stricter the bar.

- A failed claim may be retried after **two weeks** and ten posts.
- A player may hold **three** rank claims at most, each in a different allegiance (Courtless and Outlaw count as one). Only **one throne** per player.
- Once approved: a WIP profile within **3 days**, ready for review within **7 days**, or the claim lapses.
- Claimed characters start with their rank's Renown.

## Probation
Claimed ranks carry other players' stories, so they carry duties for their first **three months**:
- **Courtier, C-Class:** at least 1 in-character post a week.
- **Emissary, B-Class, A-Class:** at least 2 a week.
- **High Lord or Lady:** at least 3 a week, plus one court mission or court event each month.

Report absences in a ticket in {#submit-a-ticket}. Staff warn you once when you near the limit; the next miss loses the claim and archives the character. After three months, a High Lord or Lady's title is permanent.

## Thrones
- An empty throne is held by a staff **regent**.
- A claimed throne changes hands only by stepping down, by death, or through an **approved saga** (see {#sagas-guide}). It is never won in an ordinary challenge.
- When a throne empties, its rank claim reopens.

## Face claims
- Any **adult** face: drawn, painted or a real adult person. No minors, no art of canon characters.
- One face per character, and no face already claimed.
- Reserve a face in the **Face Claims** register for 5 days while you write; claim it on approval.

## Special claims
Forbidden magic (see {#abilities}) must be claimed before it can be learned at all.
- You need an approved character with **50 in-character posts**.
- Reply in the **Special Claims** register with a roleplay sample and your character's reason.
- Staff choose the most fitting applicant. The prize is not the magic but a **quest** for it, run by staff, death-enabled and likely deadly.
- Cauldron-Making may also be gained through the **Remade** path (see {#sagas-guide}).

## Relic claims
**The Dread Trove:** the Mask, the Harp and the Crown, scattered since the war.
- A relic is **bound** to a bearer on-screen by a ruler or regent, and only to a character with 30+ in-character posts.
- Its power fills its own slot, in three forms: **Bound**, **Awakened** and **Ascendant**. Each form advances like an ability. Reaching Substantial Mastery opens a staff-run **trial** for the next form.
- Bearers are common knowledge. Hiding a relic is nearly impossible.
- A bearer silent for a month without notice is presumed lost to the relic, and it returns to the world.

**Unique relics** (wodSouls special items) appear one at a time. When one enters play, staff open applications in the **Relic Claims** register and vote on the bearer. Another appears only once the current one is destroyed or lost. Rules for each are posted with it.
