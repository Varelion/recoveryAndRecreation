# 🏰 01 · Courts

Every character belongs to one allegiance: one of the seven courts, the **Courtless** or the **Outlaws**. Ranks are in {#ranks}; open rank claims are in {#claims}. Every empty throne is held by a **regent**, a staff NPC, until a claimant takes it.

## 🌙 Night Court
- **Seat:** {#house-of-wind}, above {#velaris}. Old nobility rules the {#hewn-city}.
- **Temper:** secretive, proud, fiercely loyal to its own. Prythian believes the Night Court is all nightmare; Velaris prefers it that way.
- **Common gifts:** darkness, shadow, mind-work, starlight.
- **Court Power:** darkness and night, and the strongest mind-gifts in Prythian.
- **Watch for:** the Illyrian war-bands, who obey whoever can make them.

## ☀️ Day Court
- **Seat:** {#city-of-day}.
- **Temper:** learned, ordered, contemptuous of ignorance. Knowledge is currency, and the court is rich.
- **Common gifts:** light, fire, spell-cleaving, truth-sense.
- **Court Power:** sunlight, and the unmaking of spells.
- **Watch for:** the sealed stacks of the {#great-library}, and the Peregryns' own politics.

## 🌅 Dawn Court
- **Seat:** {#cliffside-palace}.
- **Temper:** gentle on the surface, relentlessly inventive beneath. Its healers are welcome everywhere; its inventors are watched everywhere.
- **Common gifts:** healing, light, artifice.
- **Court Power:** healing and renewal.
- **Watch for:** the {#halls-of-healing} are neutral ground. Drawing steel there is a crime in every court.

## 🌸 Spring Court
- **Seat:** {#spring-manor}.
- **Temper:** courtly, passionate, slow to forgive. Spring guards the Wall and the holes in it.
- **Common gifts:** growth, shapeshifting, beast-speech.
- **Court Power:** growth, and the shifting of shape.
- **Watch for:** {#rosehall}, abandoned since the war, and the Calanmai fires.

## 🌊 Summer Court
- **Seat:** {#island-palace}, in {#adriata}.
- **Temper:** merchant-princes, rich and wary. Twice burned, never trusting.
- **Common gifts:** water, tide, sea-weather.
- **Court Power:** water and the tides.
- **Watch for:** the treasury everyone covets, and the pirates of the {#sapphire-reefs}.

## 🍂 Autumn Court
- **Seat:** {#forest-house}.
- **Temper:** ambitious, fiery, at war with itself. Heirs have a habit of dying young.
- **Common gifts:** fire, woodcraft, the hunt.
- **Court Power:** fire and the turning year.
- **Watch for:** the Great Hunt in the {#hunting-grounds}.

## ❄️ Winter Court
- **Seat:** {#winter-palace}.
- **Temper:** patient, stoic, long-memoried. Winter endures what kills other courts.
- **Common gifts:** ice, snow, cold.
- **Court Power:** ice and the deep cold.
- **Watch for:** the {#white-wastes}, where cold kills faster than steel.

## 🔥 Courtless
Wanderers, mercenaries, scholars, merchants and anyone who never swore to a court.
- Free to travel anywhere **outside** court cities. Entering a court's lands takes a writ, a guide or a believable reason.
- No court training (see {#abilities}), no court missions, no court protection.
- Rank by reputation: Wanderer, C-Class, B-Class, A-Class (see {#ranks}).
- A courtless character may swear to a court in play at any time.

## 🗝️ Outlaws
Oathbreakers who fled their court with its secrets, and criminals every court wants dead.
- Former rank is stripped. Standing is a **Bounty class** (see {#ranks}).
- **Death-enabled** whenever recognized by someone who would reasonably know them.
- Hunting parties may join any outlaw's thread they could reasonably track.
- Redemption exists, rarely, and only in play.
