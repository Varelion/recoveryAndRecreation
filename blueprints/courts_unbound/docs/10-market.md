# 🛒 10 · Market

**Marks** are earned by grading threads (see {#renown}). Every new character starts with **500**.

## How buying works
- Post the purchase in your purse in {#market}. Staff confirm it; the item exists from that moment.
- Roleplay what you use. Where a rule says **declare**, name the item in the thread's first post.
- Sell items back for **half** their price. An upgrade costs the new item's full price; sell the old one back.
- One Academy **or** one Keep per character, never both.

## Healing
- **Healer's draught · 500:** once per fight, brings a character back from the brink to full strength.
- **Dawn-blessed bath · 5,000** (needs a manor or better): full recovery between threads. After a DE thread, your next training thread counts ×1.5.

## Training
- **Illyrian weights · 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000** (light, medium, heavy): a training thread counts ×1.25 / ×1.5 / ×1.75. While worn, you fight one tier lower in every ability. Half effect at Substantial Mastery, none at Mastery and above. **Declare.**
- **Training circle · 10,000 / 20,000** (level I and II, needs a manor or better): a training thread at home counts ×1.75 / ×2. **Declare.**

Boosts never stack, one per thread, at most two boosted threads a week (see {#progression}).

## Schools
- **School insignia · 2,500:** needed to train under a Master's academy. Half the price goes to the Master. One Master at a time.

## Scrying
- **Scrying bowl · 5,000:** one scry for a Cauldron Shard each week, while in Prythian.
- **Seer's oil · 500:** spent by each scry, found or not.

Rules in {#sagas-guide}.

## Gear
- **Weapons · free:** pure flavor. Their benefit comes from a martial art slot.
- **Faebane · 1,000 a dose:** silences a fae's gifts for three rounds. DE threads only.
- **Ash arrows · 500 a quiver of ten:** deadly to fae. Lethal use in DE threads only.
- **Iron shackles · 1,000:** burn fae and block winnowing.
- **Wardstones · 2,000:** ward a residence. Staff tell you who crosses it.
- **Paired letters · 500:** two enchanted pages; what is written on one appears on the other.
- **Siphons · 1,000 / 2,000 / 4,000** (one stone, three, seven; Illyrians only): flavor for now. They add no power, tier or bonus.

## Travel
- **Horse · 1,000**, **warhorse · 2,500**
- **Sea passage · 300** a voyage
- **Ship · 5,000** (crew of three) · **swift ship · 10,000** (crew of six)

Travel between regions takes 24 hours of real time on foot, 12 mounted or flying (awakened wings only). Winnowing is instant within its range (see {#abilities}). A sea voyage takes 48 hours, 24 on a swift ship. Log journeys in {#travel-log}.

## Residences
Training at home, declared, counts:
- **Cottage · 2,000:** ×1.1
- **Manor · 10,000:** ×1.25. Allows a training circle and a dawn-blessed bath.
- **Estate · 20,000:** ×1.5

Register properties in {#estates}.

## Businesses
A stall, shop, inn or atelier raises the Marks from two graded threads a week set in or around it. Owner only.
- **Stall · 5,000:** +25%
- **Shop · 8,000:** +50%
- **Trading house · 20,000:** +100%
- **Guild hall · 50,000:** +200%

## War-hall, Academy, Keep
- **War-hall · 20,000:** training there counts +20% for each member of your allegiance in the thread.
- **Academy · 25,000** (upgrade from a war-hall or estate): you become a {@Master}. You gain a **signature art** in one slot and teach it. Your insignia joins the market, and half of each sale is yours. You must accept students who pass your published requirements.
- **Keep · 35,000** (upgrade from a war-hall or estate): you become lord or lady of a holding in your court, placed with staff. You gain an extra slot for a **signature art** you need not teach.

## Retainers
Companions who serve your goals: the loyal second, the sworn blade, the ward you raised.
- **Retainer I · 7,500:** primary ability three tiers below yours.
- **Retainer II · 15,000:** two tiers below.
- **Retainer III · 22,500:** one tier below.

They keep pace as you grow, and have one slot fewer than you. You may have three at most, and their levels together may not exceed 6. A retainer may act without you in your region, never against your goals, and is inactive while you are dead. Sheets go in {#retainers}.

## Guards
Fixed strength, bought for a place or a person. They never grow.
- **Sentry · 500:** Beginner, 2 slots
- **Guard · 2,500:** Novice, 2 slots
- **Veteran · 5,000:** Intermediate, 3 slots
- **Captain · 20,000:** Adept, 3 slots
- **Champion · 37,000:** High Adept, 4 slots
- **Paragon · 50,000:** Substantial Mastery, 4 slots

A retainer or guard killed in a DE thread can be restored for **half** its price.

## Trading and inheritance
- Transfers of Marks or items happen in {#trading-post}, with a link to the in-character thread that explains them. No gifts between strangers or sworn enemies.
- Between two players, across all their characters, at most **10,000 Marks** may ever change hands. An item counts at its price, on the giver's side.
- When a character retires or dies, up to **10,000 Marks** of their money or items may pass to one new character of the same player, once.

## NPCs
NPCs nobody bought have no tiers, cannot fight player characters and cannot steer the shared story. Retainers and guards are the exception: they are bought.
