# ❗ Roles

Give yourself these with the role panel below. If the panel is down, ask in {#ask-staff}.

## Pings
- {@Plot Seeker}: new plotters and plot hooks.
- {@Adventure Seeker}: missions looking for players.
- {@PVP Fiend}: duels, challenges and death-enabled fights.
- {@Saga Ping}: sagas opening and saga branches recruiting.
- {@Event Ping}: site events.

## Romance comfort
Pick one. Other players check it before writing romance with you.
- {@SFW Romance}: romance fades to black.
- {@Platonic Only}: no romance at all.
- {@NSFW Romance}: open to explicit scenes in {#after-dark}. Accepted members ({@Member}) only.

## Watching
- {@Spectator}: read the roleplay without playing.

## Given by staff
- {@Approved Player}, your allegiance and your tier: on approval.
- {@High Lord / Lady}, {@Inner Circle}, {@House Head}, {@Master}, {@Shard-bearer}: earned in play.
